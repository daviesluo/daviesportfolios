# fp22 protocol: how concentrated the day's volume is (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp21 are dead, including LS-FADE, RV-RATIO, AC-HOUR, AVG-SIZE,
PAIR-CORR, CLOSE-LOC, GAP-UP and VWAP-PREM. None of their cuts is reused,
and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No hourly bar opened on or after
2024-01-01 is pulled. The 2024-01-01 BTC daily open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the seventeen closed rounds. Also not here:

* The level of quote volume, and average trade size. Scaling every hour's
  quote volume by the same factor leaves this statistic unchanged. The
  trade count is not an input.
* The close versus the day's VWAP. No price is an input to the signal.
  VWAP-PREM stays closed, and a close under the VWAP is not a candidate.
* Lag-1 autocorrelation of hourly returns. These hourly bars contribute
  quote volume only. The lag is not reused.
* A named clock hour. The 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 season rule stays closed.
  Which hour holds the volume does not change the statistic.
* The overnight gap, where the close sits in the high-low range, and the
  width of the day's range.
* The taker share, BTC–ETH correlation, dispersion, skew, and the 7-day
  to 30-day realized-vol ratio.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**VOL-HHI.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, take the quote
volume of each of the 24 hourly bars whose open is D, D+1h, …, D+23h. All
24 must exist, and none may be negative. If the 24 sum to zero, the day is
not a print. A missing hour is a skip. The statistic is the Herfindahl of
those 24 shares: the sum of the squared share of each hour. An even day
scores 1/24. A day whose volume sits in one hour scores 1. It is known
when the 23:00 bar closes, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the Herfindahl is strictly above its own trailing-90-day
90th percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. An even day — volume spread across the hours — is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same Herfindahl are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
