# fp5: three searches, nothing to put money on, and why (review, 2026-09-26)

Cursor ran three searches on 2026-09-25/26, one per venue, on branches that are not merged: Revolut X
(`cursor/revolut-x-search-d133` at `78e3793b`), Binance (`cursor/binance-fp5-search-7bc0` at `b348842e`) and
Polymarket (`cursor/polymarket-fp5-b50c` at `31250cab`). Each was read here end to end, its key results re-run from
its committed inputs, and the claims below that decide a verdict checked in its code. None of the three found a rule
worth money. The branches stay as they are; this file is what `main` keeps of them.

## Revolut X: ~500 rules in 80 passes, nothing reached a pre-registration

* **The one positive family was a fill-model error.** Passes 78–80 booked a resting bid at the price of the
  aggressor print that hit it (`screen_pass79.py`: `entry = print_` on any sell at or below the bid; the rule texts
  say "the fill price is that later print's price"). A resting order fills at its own limit, so every trip was given
  the gap between the two for free. Booked at the limit, the every-sell queue is −541 bp (BTC −324.5 over 205 trips,
  XRP −757.5 over 59) where the branch reported +1,687; completed exits earn one tick, and the loss is one stranded
  long per book with no stop.
* **Quoting a coin book once a minute loses to adverse selection.** Both sides at the touch, refreshed each minute,
  filled only by prints strictly through the quote: −15.2 bp a round trip on BTC (4,700 trips), −26 to −35 on ETH,
  SOL and XRP. Counting prints at our price as fills as well still loses 15–33 bp, so queue priority does not rescue
  it. This is fp2's h/σ < 2.7 and the loop's own maker probes (26–35 bp adverse at 15 minutes) again.
* **The rest had no mechanism.** Daily screens on exogenous series (sunspots, river flow, Hacker News counts), 4h bar
  shapes and outside "fair values" earn about zero gross against a ~20 bp round trip; every 4h pool with 60 trips
  lost, in a year when BTC fell 24 % and ETH 37 %. About 6.8 % of the daily rules beat a null that treats clustered
  signal days as independent, which is chance. The six that cleared every numeric gate were voided (a Binance open is
  not a Revolut X fill).
* **Accounting that would hide a real edge:** 20 bp a side (twice the majors' real round trip), triangles charged 20
  bp per taker order instead of 9, and a pool summed over both books against a null drawn on one.

## Binance: ~355 rules, 25 tested out of sample, 3 "passed", none usable

* **The three passes:** BRKHI used a range known only at the close (void, caught by the search itself); PICK beat a
  null whose p95 was its own control's mean, and leaves out funding; SPQTR, a cash-and-carry on the front BTC
  quarterly, is real — all 395 out-of-sample packages positive after 40 bp — but it earns about 8.4 % a year on the
  capital it ties up (the search's "213 %/yr" counted overlapping $100 packages as one $100), down to about cash by
  2026, and a UK retail account cannot hold the future.
* **Why it failed.** A one-day rule on n≈40 trades needs about 55 bp a day over drift to clear the screen: a
  realistic 10–20 bp edge had a 1–2 % chance through both stages. The null samples without replacement
  (`common.py`: `rng.sample(pool, n)`), so where the pool was the control itself its p95 was the control's mean (31
  screens, 13 passes); overlapping holds were drawn as independent (a circular shift moves HI10 from a pass to the
  90th percentile); listing-auction bars sat in the pool. 13 of the 21 out-of-sample failures that traded made money in
  2024 and lost in 2025–26: that is market exposure, which the both-halves test rightly rejected.

## Polymarket: 58 taker rules pre-registered, 49 scored, none passed

* Together −$3,008 on 7,826 out-of-sample trades (−5.2 % a dollar; fees 3.5 %). One rule of 49 beat its null, which
  is what chance gives.
* **Why it failed.** Every rule was a taker against books that already price bookmaker lines, weather models and
  Deribit: fp4 had shown the weather market beats a real forecast, and fp5's twelve temperature rules used none.
  Football "fair values" from 6–8 matches carry about ±15 points of error against the 1–4 points a mispricing might
  be. 28 of 49 fell short of 80 trades, which event counts would have shown before any freeze. The early input
  scripts charged fees at a 0.07 rate on markets that had none (`vol_inputs.py` ignores `feesEnabled` and the
  schedule's exponent; `rate_of`, used later, is right); re-scored with the real fees no verdict changes.
* **The one near-miss, DRAWBASE** (buy the draw before Premier League kickoffs): +$362 on 100 trades, above its
  null, but it failed both halves and the month cap, and its profit is one season in which draws came 32–33 % of the
  time against a 24.5¢ price.

## What the three share

Taker rules against prices that already know more; many hypotheses a day with no power check (rounds of "at least
eight"); nulls narrower than the data; fill and fee models wrong in the direction of the result; and fills priced at
a venue this account does not trade. The effects that are real and measured on these venues are 0.2–5 bp, against a
20 bp round trip.

## What is worth running, in order

1. **RW-E** (Polymarket, pre-registered 2026-09-26, `2026-09-26-polymarket-rw-end-prereg.md`): RW without the
   markets that end on the day they are quoted, judged with RW on 2026-10-09 by exact replay of RW's stored tables.
   The one lead with a measured cause in live data.
2. **PR6, USD stablecoins at par** (Revolut X): 0 % bids and asks 1–3 ticks either side of 1.0000 on USDC-USD and
   USDT-USD, scored on twelve months of keyless public prints with PR5's frozen simulator (fills only on prints
   strictly through a quote, at most $100 and 10 % of the minute's volume). The mechanism that passed as PR3/PR5,
   with a fair value that does not move; fp5 scored it only on hourly candles, which cannot say whether it fills.
3. **Funding crowding as a spot trade** (Binance signal, Revolut X execution): the frozen UZERO / USOFR signals
   (BTCUSDT funding below zero, or below the 8h SOFR carry) → a BTC spot long for 48 h at 9 bp a side. Their
   2024–26 and 2020–22 data were never used, so a clean test with a circular-shift null is still possible.
4. **DRAW-X** (Polymarket): DRAWBASE unchanged on La Liga, the Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and Serie A, keyless; at least 300
   trades, positive in 3 of 4 leagues. Settles the one taker near-miss either way.
5. **Record Revolut X's UK order book** for four weeks (the stablecoin books, with XRP-USD and BTC-USD as controls),
   with a queue model frozen before any of it is read: on pegged books the queue, not price movement, decides fills,
   and "no book history" stopped both fp2 and fp5.
6. **PR5's live path** after its dry-run (Davies' go): each live fill against the paper engine's call for the same
   quote-minute — the check every quote result so far rests on.

Lower: STREAK on ETH/SOL/XRP/AVAX as a filter inside trend-4h's backtester; a maker version of the Bitcoin ladder;
carry on a venue this account can use (it needs one first).

**Rules for any next round:** a power check first (expected trades and the smallest edge the test can see); at most
ten hypotheses a round, Holm-corrected; nulls drawn with replacement or by circular shift, on distinct entry dates;
annualised on the capital a rule ties up; funding on every perp leg; fees from each market's own schedule; a resting
order filled at its limit, and only by a print through it; fills priced where the account trades.
