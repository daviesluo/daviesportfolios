## Not this search

* The quote-weighted hour. VOL-CLOCK stays closed, including its later years and both neighbours. Quote is not stored. Moving base from the high's hour to another hour changes this share and would leave a quote clock unchanged.
* The hour index of the high. HIGH-HOUR stays closed. A late high with no base scores near zero here and scores 23 there.
* Quote in the hours that closed above their own open. UP-VOL stays closed. The close and the open are not stored, so a down hour can hold the high and the base.
* The Herfindahl of hourly quote, and the max-hour share of the return. VOL-HHI and RET-HHI stay closed. One hour can hold the high's base while the quote is spread out, and this share does not read the return.
* The rise in base volume. BASE-CHG stays closed, including its later years and both neighbours. The day's total base cancels in the share.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**HIGH-BASE.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, all 24 hours must exist. Take the earliest hour whose high is strictly above every earlier high. Divide that hour's base volume by the day's base volume. Base must be non-negative and the day's base must be positive. A tie keeps the earlier hour. A zero-base day is not a print. The known 13:00 hole drops that day. It is not filled in. The statistic is known when D closes, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. A high whose hour held less of the base is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto
that can only reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored
on this screen.
