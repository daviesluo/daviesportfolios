Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp45 are dead, including LS-FADE, BODY and its out-of-sample
result, BAL-CHG, IMPACT and its out-of-sample result, PEAK, and the
screens fp30 through fp45. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is

candidate. PEAK's close under the prior 30-day high, and its 80th, are not
candidates. STREAK and OPEN-DRIVE failed their later years. Their 80th and

and neither is BAL-CHG's 80th. TURN's count stays 30. A rule rejected

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No daily bar opens after 2024-01-01. The 2024-01-01 quote and trade count are stored as 0. The open is kept.

## Not this search

* The level of quote per trade. AVG-SIZE stays closed. A day whose size equals yesterday scores 0, however large the size.
* The rise in the trade count, and the level of the count. TRD-CHG stays closed. Doubling quote and count together leaves this statistic at 0 while the count rose.
* Absolute return per unit of quote volume. AMIHUD stays closed.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**AVG-CHG.** BTCUSDT daily bars. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, average size is quote volume divided by the trade count. The statistic is today's size divided by yesterday's size, minus 1. Both sizes must be positive. Yesterday is exactly one day earlier. A missing day is a skip. A day that did not grow stays in the history. The statistic is known when day D closes, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. A day whose average size did not rise is not scored. The 2024-01-01 quote and trade count are stored as 0, so that bar is not a print.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto
that can only reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored
on this screen.
