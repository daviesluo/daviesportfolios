## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. This file adds none.
Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print
and is not filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of a one-day short on every in-screen day, failed break or not. The strategy is that short only on the day after the high broke yesterday's high and the close finished back under it, so it is a subset. Daily opens through 2024-01-01 may finish a 2023 entry. They are not entries. The funding cash is not added. A later open is a bug. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net
above zero, and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**FAILSH.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, the high trades strictly above yesterday's high and the close finishes strictly under that high. A close equal to the high does not fire. A high that does not clear yesterday does not fire. This is not a long on the break day, and it is not a ratio of today's high to yesterday's high.

The next day is short from the BTCUSDT daily open to the next open. The funding cash is not added. The short sells at 10 bps under the entry and buys back at 10 bps above the later open. One day is the hold written here.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01 is scored. If
this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the hold or
the cut.
