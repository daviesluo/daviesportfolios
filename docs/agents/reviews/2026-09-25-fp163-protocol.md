## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. This file adds none.
Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print
and is not filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of this same target-or-close payoff on every in-screen day, whether or not the taker ratio rose. The strategy is that payoff only after the ratio rose, so it is a subset. Spot bars through 2024-01-01 may finish a 2023 entry. They are not entries. Taker prints stop on 2023-12-31. Two percent is not refit. A later open is a bug. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net
above zero, and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**TAKTGT.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, the last taker long/short volume ratio is strictly above the first print of that UTC day. One is not the cut. The ratio is not compared with yesterday, and it is not ranked. A flat path does not fire. A falling path does not fire. This is not a long from the next open to the close after the last print is above one, and it is not a cross up through one.

Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. If that day's high reaches 2% above the open, the exit is that target. Otherwise the exit is the close. The fill is fp5's `net_return` from the open to that exit. Two percent is the rule written here, not a width chosen after a return. A stop under the open is not this rule.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01 is scored. If
this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the hold or
the cut.
