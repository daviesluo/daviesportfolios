# fp12 protocol: BTC dominance return (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp11 are dead. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No dominance file from 2024 onward
is pulled.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the seven closed rounds. Also not here:
BTC's share of BTC+ETH quote volume, ETH's own return, BTC's own 4h trend,
and the lower tail of this series. If this rule fails, the volume-share rule
is not reopened and the sign is not flipped.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. A dominance bar opened on or after 2024-01-01 is refused, except
the 2024-01-01 BTC open, which may be an exit print. The null is fp5's: 200
draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, drawn from every in-screen BTC 8h hold.
A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above the
null.

## The idea

**DOM-UP.** Binance USDT-M 8h klines for `BTCDOMUSDT`, monthly files on
`data.binance.vision`, October 2022 through December 2023. A missing month
is a skip, not a zero. At each bar whose open is t, with t strictly before
2024-01-01, the return is that bar's close divided by the close of the bar
opened exactly 8h earlier, minus 1. Both closes must be positive and both
bars must exist. The return is known at t+8h, and that is its timestamp. It
fires when the return is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[t+8h−90 days, t+8h)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter BTCUSDT spot at t+8h. Exit at t+16h.
BTC spot's own return is not an input. The lower tail is not scored. This is
the traded dominance contract, not the volume ratio closed in fp7.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. Neighbouring quantiles, the
80th and the 95th of the same return, are a veto that can only reject. They
are not scored on this screen. 2023 is not part of that bar. If this rule
fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the cut.
