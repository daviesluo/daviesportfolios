## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. A day with no EOHSummary file is not a print and is not filled
in. No file dated 2024-01-01 or later is requested. The 2024-01-01 daily
open may be an exit print. A later open is a bug. The null is fp5's:
200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, drawn from every in-screen BTCUSDT
hold of one day. The pool is BTC's, because the fill is BTC's. A pass is at
least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**STRIKE.** BTCUSDT options. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, take the last hour present in that day's EOHSummary file. The statistic is the count of distinct strike prices whose `openinterest_contracts` is positive. A call and a put at the same strike count once. A second expiry at the same strike counts once. A non-positive open interest does not add a strike. No such strike is not a print. The strike is parsed from the symbol, not from the glued strike field. The premium in USDT is not an input.

The last hour is the largest hour label in that file. A file that ends
before hour 23 still uses the hour it does contain. The raw contract rows
are stored. The statistic is computed when the screen is scored.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic, at this same
one-day hold, are named as neighbours. They are a veto that can only
reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this
screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01. If this rule
fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the window, the hold,
or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto that can only
reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.
