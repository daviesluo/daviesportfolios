## Not this search

* BTC's share of BTC+ETH quote volume, its lower tail, and the change in that share. That screen stays closed. ETH is not in this round because ETH quote over BTC quote is the same ordering as the lower tail of that share.
* The coin's own quote level, with BTC left out of the ratio. A volume climax on an 8h drop stays closed.
* The trade-count level, and quote per trade. Those stay closed.
* Taker-buy fields. Those stay closed.
* Any hourly open, high, low, close, or hourly volume share from fp30 through fp94. That family stays closed.
* The reciprocal, BTC quote over ADA quote. That is the lower tail, and it is not a separate rule.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen ADAUSDT daily hold of one day. The pool is
ADA's, because the fill is ADA's. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**ADA-Q.** ADAUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, divide ADA's quote volume by BTCUSDT's quote volume. Either quote non-positive, or either bar missing, is not a print. The opens are stored for the fill and are not inputs. No hourly bar is read. The statistic is known when D closes, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next ADAUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. A day ADA was quiet versus BTC is not a separate rule.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on ADAUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any later-year price. If this rule fails, it is
dead and the next search does not inherit the window or the cut. The 80th
and the 95th of this statistic are a veto that can only reject, and only
after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.
