# fp6 study: Binance's derivatives, five hypotheses as frozen — none passes

Phase 2 of the fourth Binance search (Davies, 2026-09-26). The five pre-registrations were frozen on `main` by
`d856fd2e` (`reviews/2026-09-26-fp6-prereg-family.md` and its five files); this is the test exactly as frozen, run
2026-09-26 22:30 → 2026-09-27 UTC by a research agent on its own branch. Nothing was placed, no key was read, every
input is public and keyless.

## The verdict

**No hypothesis passes.** Two clear their Holm step (H5, H6) and fail other conditions of their own bar; three fail
their Holm step and most of their bar.

| # | rule | Holm | conditions that fail | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H2 | ETH spot against its quarterly, ≥ 8 % net basis, to delivery | fails (p 0.810) | 1 (5.67 %/yr < 8 %), 4 (last year 1.16 %), 5 (5.29 % with costs doubled), 6 (April 2024 is 49.6 % of the P&L) | **FAIL** |
| H3 | spot/perp carry on the top-40 altcoins, 3-day funding ≥ 25 % | fails (p 0.9995) | 1 (2.58 %/yr), 3 (second half −0.56 %/yr), 4 (last year −0.16 %), 5 (1.18 %), 6 (July 2024 is 50.5 %) | **FAIL** |
| H4 | the live trend rule long and short on perpetuals | fails (p 0.961) | 1 and 3 in all four evaluations (the worst window and window D get worse), 4 in the two `shipped` evaluations | **FAIL** |
| H5 | short the perpetual after "Binance Will Delist …" | **clears** (p 0.0005) | 5 (April 2026 is 46.6 % of the P&L) | **FAIL** |

## Access, restated

Every one of the five has a derivative leg. A UK-registered retail account may not be sold one (FCA PS20/10 in force
2021-01-06; COBS 22.6.5R); Binance has restricted UK users' futures by investor category since 2022 and offered them
nothing new since 2023-10-16; it told EU users it would stop serving them from 2026-07-01, and what an Irish-registered
account may do now is not public. **No result here would have made any of them available to this account**: that is
Davies' and Binance's call, not the study's. Sources and dates: the family file.

## Speed at one second (Davies' standing rule, 2026-09-26; added after the freeze, descriptive)

pg_cron 1.6.4 runs a job every 1–59 s, so a study prices speed at 1 s. The frozen verdicts above stand as frozen.

* **H2**: decided at 00:00 on the previous closes and held to delivery; a 1 s loop sees nothing new before the open it
  fills at, so speed changes nothing.
* **H3**: decided on funding settled over the last three days (settlements come every 8 h, hourly when capped) and
  filled at the open; its guard is already priced at its level inside the day. Speed changes nothing.
* **H4**: decided on 4-hour closes and filled at the next open, stops priced at their level inside the bar; a 1 s
  loop sees the same close at the same instant. Speed changes nothing.
* **H5 and H6** react to events a daily loop enters hours late (H5: 15–21 h after the announcement; H6: up to a day
  after the first trade). Their 1 s arms (`speed_1s.json`, `score_1s.py`) enter 1 s after the event — H5 after the
  announcement's CMS `releaseDate`, H6 after the perpetual's first trade — at the first USDⓈ-M aggTrade at or after
  that instant, at the frozen costs (0.05 % taker, 3.56 bp), with the frozen exit, stop (1.5 × the arm's own entry,
  read on the entry day's remaining trades and minutes, then on daily highs) and funding. The trades were read from
  `data.binance.vision`'s daily aggTrades (577 files, 5.5 GB, each streamed once, checked against its published
  sha256 and not stored; `fetch_1s.py`), with each entry day's 1-minute klines.

| arm | fills | frozen daily entry | 1 s entry | 1 s − frozen | events better / worse | stops, 1 s / frozen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H5 delist short | 62 of 67 | +$265.80 | **+$1,142.00** | **+$876.20** | 53 / 14 | 10 / 13 |
| H6 new-perpetual short | 515 of 515 | +$1,736.25 | **−$3,176.14** | **−$4,912.39** | 284 / 231 | 215 / 175 |

* **H5 at 1 s gains.** The first print after an announcement sits a median 23 % above the next day's open (84 % of the
  fills are above it): the daily loop misses most of the fall. The arm's halves are +$133.53 and +$1,008.46, its best
  month April 2026 at 21.3 % of the P&L, 38.2 %/yr on the $800 — its sum, halves, month share and return would all
  clear the frozen lines (its null and its doubled costs were not run). **It was seen after the freeze and is not
  evidence**: a rule built on it needs its own pre-registration, and a real fill in a delisting's first seconds crosses
  a book far wider than the frozen 3.56 bp, if Binance lets a new short open at all (the prereg's caveat: it may cut a
  delisting contract's limits). Five events have no fill: SRM, BTS, IDEX, SXP and MDT had already stopped trading.
* **H6 at 1 s loses.** The first print after a listing is a median 1.3 % above the next day's open, and the listing
  day's squeezes stop 67 shorts that same day (8 within their first minute): 215 stops against the daily entry's 175,
  −$13,548.85 on the stopped shorts. Speed makes H6 far worse.

## Readings and deviations

Where a prereg was silent, the scorer took one reading before its result was seen, stated in its docstring:
* P&L of day t is the change of the marks from t's open to t+1's open; the closing trades at 2026-09-25's open count
  on the last window day (H2, H3).
* H2: a new quarterly sold on a delivery day pays the farther half-spread (the delivering contract is still listed that
  day). A rolled package keeps the spot, so its notional follows ETH (1,468 on the 2024-03-29 roll).
* H3: the guard's entry price is the perpetual's open on the entry day; a leg with no bar takes its last close; the
  null's guard package is held through its guard day.
* H4: a stop's funding exit is the stop bar's start; SOL's 49 two-hourly settlements are marked at the open of the
  bar that contains them; a null episode must end inside the span; the 5th percentile is the house quantile.
* H5, H6: the stop's entry price is the entry day's open; a stopped short's funding runs to the end of the stop day
  (the costlier reading, as H3's frozen guard); a donor is shorted over the event's planned span under its own stop;
  the capital of condition 6 is phase 1's peak of planned holds (the peak of the holds taken: 8 and 26).

Deviations from the frozen text, all in descriptive parts: H3's USDC-margined twins were not computed; SPQTR's rule on
ETH is re-implemented from its protocol text; the H2 scorer also reads `backtests/fund/inputs/sofr.json.gz` for the
descriptive SOFR mean. No primary, bar or verdict was changed.

## What this cannot show

Access (above). Custody and counterparty risk on Binance. Intraday squeezes and liquidations beyond what daily highs
show (H3, H5, H6); spreads in a coin's worst hours (one evening's books, doubled in the cost conditions); the
perpetual's own price in H4 (the spot tapes' opens). That Binance keeps listing quarterlies, delisting coins the same
way, or listing perpetuals at 2025's pace.
