## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print and is not
filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of a long from the open to the close on every in-screen day. It is not an open-to-next-open hold. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero,
and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**TAKSESS.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, read the last `sum_taker_long_short_vol_ratio` of the UTC day. It fires when that ratio is strictly above one. One is parity, not a percentile. The first print is not stored. The path through the day is not stored. A ratio of one does not fire. A ratio under one does not fire.

Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open and sell that same day's close. The fill is fp5's `net_return` between those two prices. The next day's open is not the exit. The taker-ratio level, and the day-over-day jump in the ratio, stay closed and are not this cut.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01 is scored. If
this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the hold or
the cut.
