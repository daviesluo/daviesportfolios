# fp322 protocol: WBRAT (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. §3.369 is the
retrospective this round answers. This rule does not buy because a close
finished under another venue's close. Fair value and the finished close are
both ETH per WBETH. The discount is `(fair − close) / close`, in basis
points. The close is the previous day's bar on WBETHETH. It has finished
before the entry. The entry open is the fill and is not in the subtraction.
The entry day's own close is not an input. High and low are not stored.

The fair value is Binance's own published exchange rate for the token, not
another venue's last trade, and not the ETH spot, coin-margined or USDT
book. A rate stamped on the entry, or later, is not used. A book ticker is
not read. The position is one leg. The count stays 30. A mean that clears
the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less is not taken out of sample. Later years
are scored only when the three screen bars pass and the mean net is strictly
more than 20 bps above the edge-off trade, and only in a later file.
LS-FADE's 60-trade reproduction, the control, doubled costs, and the rule
that no month is above 40% of the profit are not relaxed. A Binance daily
open is not a Revolut X fill.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption and is not a testing row. 2023 is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. Public
reads only. Ten basis points a side. One hundred dollars stands on the
one leg, so the dollar result is one hundred times that leg's net.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on the one leg. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC.
An exit may be the 2024-01-01 open when the hold reaches that day. That
open is not an entry and not a signal. A day with no print is not filled
in. The April 2023 monthly kline was not published. It is not invented.
The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of the edge-off trade
named below. When that trade has more fills than this rule, the cutoff is
the house p95 of samples of this rule's count. It is not replaced by that
trade's own mean. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and
mean net above that null.

A missing previous close, or a rate that was not yet published, is neither
trade. It is not the edge-off set.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE stays as written: both later sub-windows
positive, above a fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30
trades, no month above 40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more
than 4% a year on the $100 it locks. The 60-trade reproduction of LS-FADE
is not relaxed. A pass that clears the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less stays
inside 2023. If this rule fails, it is dead. This file does not arm a testing
row.

## Where the series is published

The exchange rate is
`GET /bapi/earn/v1/public/pos/cftoken/project/exchange-rate/history?projectId=BETH`
on `https://www.binance.com`. `exchangeRate` is ETH per WBETH. `calcDate` is
the publication, in milliseconds. `apr` is not stored. A stamp that is not
strictly earlier than the entry is not used. The support-page FAQ at
`https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/c2edfefdad214259b984730a0cb760ec`
returned an empty body on the day this was written, so it is not the source.

The history that was read has 1251 rows. Rates with `calcDate` on or after
2024-01-01 are not stored. The frozen shape is 252 rates, one per day from
2023-04-24 (`1682294400000`, rate `1.000000000000`) through 2023-12-31
(`1703980800000`, rate `1.026784871806`).

The daily bar of `WBETHETH` supplies the previous close and the two opens.
It is read from the vision spot daily kline. The first stored bar is
2023-05-12 (`1683849600000`). April is absent. High and low are not stored.
No book ticker is read. `https://fapi.binance.com` and
`https://dapi.binance.com` are not the host. The vision host is
`https://data.binance.vision`.

## The idea

**WBRAT.** Fair value is the published WBETH exchange rate, ETH per WBETH.
The previous close of WBETHETH is at least 25 basis points under that rate.
Buy the spot book for two days. The other trade buys it for two days when
the discount is below 25 basis points. Same side. The edge is turned off.
Not the opposite side. Not an unconditional hold. The entry open is not an
input. One leg.

Thresholds counted before this freeze, smallest first, were 1, 5, 10, 25,
50, 100, 200, 500, 1000 and 2000 basis points. The hold counted for the
shipped line is two days, the shortest hold that is not one day. At 1 basis
point the cheap set was the longer one, 209 against 23. At 5 basis points it
was 188 against 44. At 10 basis points it was 147 against 85. The short side
was not taken. Twenty-five basis points is the first line at which the cheap
set has at least 30 spans and the edge-off set is at least as long. Fifty
basis points has 4 spans and was not substituted.

A count of spans, taken before this freeze, with no profit computed: the rule
has 43 spans and the edge-off trade has 189. One fillable day has neither
print. Fillable days were 233. The comparison is decimal, not binary
floating point: the widest edge-off discount is 24.8594 basis points and the
smallest rule discount is 25.0092. The 2023-05-13 entry, which is not a
return, uses the 2023-05-12 close `0.99710000` against the rate
`1.001771431910` stamped at 2023-05-12 00:00 UTC, strictly before that
entry. The close is 46.8502 basis points under the rate. The other series that were counted and not shipped are in §3.370.
