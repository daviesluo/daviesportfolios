# Pre-registration PR5: PR3's rule on the whole public trade tape of Revolut X's USDC/GBP and USDT/GBP books

Written 2026-09-23, before any return, fill, position or order count of this rule on the new data was computed.
Frozen by the sha256 in `prereg_pr5_revx_gbp_stable_long.sha256` and the UTC time in
`prereg_pr5_revx_gbp_stable_long.frozen_at`. Script: `scripts/pr5_sim.py`; its two settings are in `config.json`
(hash below), frozen with this file.

## Why a fifth test

PR3 (resting 0 % maker quotes 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 % either side of interbank on Revolut X's USDC/GBP and USDT/GBP books)
passed its bar on 13.25 out-of-sample days (+$4.68 on $1,200, 57 round trips), because it read the venue's
1-minute candles, which exist for 28 days only; its distances were chosen after seeing where those 28 days' volume
traded. Revolut X serves its whole public trade history without a key
(`GET /api/1.0/public/trades/all?symbol=…&start_date=…&end_date=…`, ≤ 1 day a window). Everything before
2026-08-26 00:00 UTC is data PR3 never saw. This test runs PR3's rule, unchanged except where the data forces a
change, on that data.

## Windows

* **PRIMARY** (the bar): USDC/GBP 2025-11-26 00:00 → 2026-08-26 00:00 UTC; USDT/GBP 2025-12-16 00:00 →
  2026-08-26 00:00 UTC. The simulation starts fresh (every rung idle) at each book's start and runs to
  2026-09-23 00:00; a position counts in the window where it opened and is followed to its exit.
* **PR3 period, recomputed on prints** (descriptive): a fresh simulation from 2026-08-26 00:00 to 2026-09-23 00:00,
  reported whole and in PR3's halves (IS → 2026-09-09 18:00, OOS after).
* **Months**: every calendar month (UTC), by the month a position opened.

## The rule — PR3's, unchanged

The loop acts at the start of minute t on data up to t−1; an order placed or re-priced at t is live from t+1; a fill
in minute m is seen at the turn of m+1, where the exit is placed (live from m+2).
* Interbank `X(t)`: the latest FX minute close whose minute started in [t−10 min, t−1 min]; none → no entry quotes
  (resting entry quotes are withdrawn) and no new exit prices that minute. The FX source has no quotes at the weekend
  and on 25 Dec / 1 Jan, so those are dark, as in PR3.
* `fairU(S,t)`: the 24-hour median of the S/USD UK book (proxy below); `fair(S,t) = fairU(S,t) / X(t)` (GBP per coin).
* Rungs per book and side `k ∈ {0.10 %, 0.20 %, 0.30 %}`; bid `fair·(1−k)` rounded down to 0.0001, ask `fair·(1+k)`
  rounded up; $100 each (converted at X). Capital 2 books × 2 sides × 3 rungs × $100 = $1,200.
* Re-pricing: a rung is re-priced when fair has moved more than 0.05 % since it was priced; every placement and
  re-price counts one order.
* Exit: a post-only order at the current fair (a long exits with an ask rounded up, a short with a bid rounded down),
  re-priced on 0.05 % moves, filling by the same test as an entry. Not filled within 24 h of the fill minute: closed
  as a taker.
* One position per rung at a time; the rung is re-placed at the turn after its position closes.
* P&L in GBP, converted to USD at X of the exit minute (the last known X if none that minute).

## What the data forces — every change, and why

1. **Fills from prints.** A resting bid at p fills only on a print strictly below p whose timestamp is at or after
   the start of the order's live minute; an ask only on a print strictly above p. Fill at p. (PR3 used the close of a
   traded minute because it had candles; the prints are the trades themselves. A print strictly through p proves the
   level p was swept.)
2. **Size**: min($100, 10 % of the quote volume printed on that book in the fill print's minute, GBP → USD at X).
   (PR3's cap on candle volume, which is the prints' volume.)
3. **Post-only.** When an order goes live, the last print before that instant decides whether the market was already
   through it: a bid at p is refused if that print was below p, or at p and a BUY (the ask sat at p); an ask at p if
   the print was above p, or at p and a SELL. A refused order cannot fill. At each later turn the loop re-places it
   (one order) if the last print is no longer through its price — re-targeted to the current fair if fair moved more
   than 0.05 % meanwhile, without an order — and it goes live the minute after, checked again. This applies to entries
   and exits. (A post-only order that would cross is refused by the venue; PR3 could not see this and 22 of its 57
   OOS fills came in minutes that opened through the quote.)
4. **Fair value.** PR3's `fairU` was the median of the USD book's 1-minute UK closes over [t−1440, t−1], which exist
   for 28 days only. Three proxies that exist for the whole span were compared with PR3's own fair on every minute of
   2026-08-27 → 09-23 before any P&L (`results/fair_check.json`): the median of the UK prints (F1), the time-weighted
   median of the last UK print (F2), and **the median of the closes of the UK hourly candles lying wholly inside
   [t−24 h, t) (F3)**. F3 equals PR3's fair exactly in 94.7 % (USDC-USD) and 86.8 % (USDT-USD) of minutes, p95
   |dev| 1 bp; F1 and F2 in 32–43 %, p95 2 bps. **F3 is used** (PR3's closes, like the hourly closes, are mostly quote
   mids: 97–98 % of the USD books' minutes carry no trade).
5. **Interbank rate.** PR3 used Yahoo's 1-minute GBPUSD=X, which reaches back about 30 days. Chosen before any P&L
   (`results/fx_checks.json`) among the public minute sources that cover the span: **Exness's tick archive (minute
   close = mid of the minute's last quote)**. Against Yahoo's hourly GBPUSD=X over the span (5,026 hours, weekdays):
   median −0.29 bps, median |dev| 0.43, p95 |dev| 1.66, p99 2.62. Against FXCM's independent 1-minute bid/ask archive
   where it exists (2025-11 → 2026-05-01, 2026-08-09 → 09-18; 202,482 minutes): median |dev| 0.11 bps, p95 0.37.
   Against PR3's own Yahoo 1-minute closes (24,117 minutes): median |dev| 0.37 bps, p95 1.25 (lag 0 is the best
   alignment). Rejected: Kraken's GBP/USD tape (2.07 M prints; against Yahoo hourly median −1.15 bps, median |dev|
   1.46, p95 3.85; against PR3's Yahoo 1-minute closes median −2.34 bps; it trades through the weekend, so it has no
   FX closure); FXCM (no file for 2026-W18 → W31); Dukascopy (refused this machine, HTTP 429 then connection reset).
   The weekend and holiday rule is PR3's, applied to the chosen source.
6. **Taker stop price.** PR3 closed at the minute's candle close; with prints, the last print at or before the end of
   that minute, × (1 ∓ (0.09 % + 0.0067 %)) as PR3.
7. **"Traded minute"** (the null's pool) = a minute with at least one print on that book.

## Arms

1. **Primary**: as written.
2. **Stress** (must stay > 0): taker exits at 18 bps + the full spread (1.34 bps); every fill, entry and exit, needs a
   print one more tick (0.0001) through the price.
3. Descriptive, not in the bar: fills only from the opposite aggressor (a bid only on a SELL print, an ask only on a
   BUY print); the rule without the post-only refusal (the brief's fill rule alone); full $100 fills; by book, by
   rung, by side; capacity at $300 / $1,000 / $3,000 rungs with the same 10 % cap.

## Null

PR3's random-time twins: each PRIMARY round trip is replaced by an entry on the same book and side at the last print
of a uniformly drawn PRIMARY-window traded minute of that book that has an FX rate, sized min($100, 10 % of that
minute's printed quote volume), followed by the same exit machinery (post-only exit at fair from the next turn, the
same refusal rule, the 24-hour stop). 2,000 draws, `random.Random(20260923)`.

## The bar (PRIMARY; all must hold)

1. P&L > 0.
2. P&L > the null's 95th percentile.
3. Stress P&L > 0.
4. At least 60 round trips.
5. Positive in at least two thirds of calendar months: every calendar month that overlaps the primary window counts
   (2025-11 … 2026-08, ten months, the first and last partial); a month with no round trip is not positive; so at
   least 7 of 10.
6. No single month more than 40 % of the total P&L.

Pass → a paper-test specification (`paper_test_spec.md`). Fail → no paper test; the report says which condition failed.

## Reported separately (not part of the bar)

Return per year on the capital the quotes lock (P&L ÷ capital-years: $600 per book over its primary days) against
cash at ~4 %/yr; capacity (the arms above); orders per day (mean and the busiest day) against Revolut X's 1,000 a day.

## Determinism

`scripts/pr5_sim.py results/pr5_run1.json` and `… results/pr5_run2.json`; the two files must be byte-identical.

## What this cannot show

* The order book is not in the public record: whether a post-only order would have been accepted is judged from the
  last print, and queue position beyond "strictly through" and the 10 % cap is not modelled.
* A print strictly through p proves the level was swept only if the order was in the book at p; the refusal rule is
  the only protection against a quote that the market had already passed without printing.
* The venue's candles miss a few prints that its tape carries (the completeness check lists them); they are counted
  as trades here.
* The gap the rule harvests shrank through the year; a pass on nine months does not show that September's gap pays.
