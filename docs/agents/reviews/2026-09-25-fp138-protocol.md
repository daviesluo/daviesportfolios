# fp138 protocol: the width of the BTC premium (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5 through
fp135 are dead or are other files in this search. None of their cuts is reused,
and no sign is flipped. A positive neighbour is not a candidate. The count stays
30. The window is not shrunk. This file reads the BTCUSDT premium-index daily bar, and only its high and its low. It does not read the close, a funding print, or an hourly price bar.

The series behind this rule is long enough that the frozen 90-day, 90-print
rule has a full run of 2023 days that can fire. A missing day is not filled in.
A point on or after 2024-01-01 is not stored. September 2022 is not requested.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any daily open after 2024-01-01
is scored. 2023 is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return` on BTCUSDT. Public reads only.

## Not this search

* PREM-REV. The cheap side of the basis, at a fixed 10 basis points, stays closed.
* The rich side of the basis, and the level of the premium. The close is not stored.
* Either signed tail of the premium. This statistic is a width, so it is not negative.
* Mark and index closes. Those are not read.
* UTXO, BLKSZ, MEDCONF, WIKI, BLKCNT, OUTBTC, UTXORNG and CONFR. No on-chain chart and no calendar series is read.
* OPTOI, OPTVOL, PCOI, PCVOL, STRIKE, GAMMA, DTE and DELTA. No option file is read. The reciprocal of a zero-fill rule is not scored.
* MEMBYTE. No mempool chart is read.
* FRNG-2 through FRNG-14. Eight holds of one funding range are one rule.
* SOL-Q through LTC-Q, and AVAX-Q. No alt quote is read.
* Hourly price bars from fp30 through fp94. No hourly price bar is read.
* Coin-margined liquidation snapshots start on 2023-06-25. The USDT-margined archive has no liquidation prefix. Neither is scored, and the window is not shrunk to make them fit.
* A neighbour quantile. The 80th and the 95th are a veto only after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.
* Another hold of this same signal. The hold in this file is one day.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. A day with no print is not a print and is not filled in. The
2024-01-01 daily open may be an exit print. A later open is a bug. The null
is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, drawn from every in-screen
BTCUSDT hold of one day. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero,
and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**BASERNG.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, subtract the premium-index daily low from the daily high. The premium may be negative. High must be at least low. A zero width stays in the history. The close is not stored, so the level cannot be scored from this file. A missing day is not a print and is not filled in. Both raw numbers are stored, and the width is computed when the screen is scored.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic, at this same
one-day hold, are named as neighbours. They are a veto that can only
reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this
screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01. If this rule
fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the window, the hold,
or the cut.
