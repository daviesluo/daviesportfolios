账户来做", and on the tightening: "这里说的这个做市商的盈利水平如何？我们有机会赢他吗？".

**Not blind, research only.** No order, config, migration or frozen file was touched. Every pound below is a replay's (paper).
LIVE appears only as counts, shares and bps; the list of prints that filled LIVE (used to take them out of the tape in §2) is

## 0. Answers first

**1. A (one tick inside the maker on Revolut X, hedged at once on Coinbase) can be fully automated for the trading, and is
worth paper. Run without transfers, it earns about £0.7 a day on £600 and £1.1 a day on £1,200 (USDT-GBP). The first pass's
£10 a day was too high: those inventories never ran out and it paid nothing to move coins.**
- **The hedge does not need to be fast.** Hedging at Coinbase 0, 15, 30, 60 or 90 s after the Revolut X fill gives the same
  edge: USDT-GBP 7.22–7.31 bps a fill, USDC-GBP 6.17–6.30 bps (§1.2). The profit is the basis between the venues (Revolut X's
  books sit about 5 bps under Coinbase's), and the basis does not move in a minute. A once-a-minute executor is enough.
- **The money is the inventory, not the speed.** Every fill moves pounds and coins between the venues. Moving them back costs
  £3 plus the network fee each time on Revolut X's side. Done often it eats everything: at £5,000 with £500 batches, USDC-GBP
  nets −2.2 bps a fill and USDT-GBP −2.3. Left alone, the two sides' flows partly balance by themselves and the books keep
  trading:

  | book | capital (both venues) | £ a fill | fills a day | net bps a fill (all costs) | £ a day | % a year | days > 0 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | USDT-GBP | £600 | 100 | 16.9 | +6.3 | **0.72** | 43.6 | 15/16 |
  | USDT-GBP | £1,200 | 100 | 24.8 | +6.7 | **1.08** | 32.8 | 15/16 |
  | USDT-GBP | £5,000 | 500 | 24.9 | +6.3 | **3.25** | 23.7 | 15/16 |
  | USDT-GBP | £20,000 | 500 | 31.4 | +7.0 | 4.60 | 8.4 | 15/16 |
  | USDC-GBP | £600 | 100 | 11.2 | +5.0 | 0.30 | 18.0 | 15/15 |
  | USDC-GBP | £1,200 | 100 | 14.2 | +5.7 | 0.44 | 13.4 | 15/15 |
  | USDC-GBP | £5,000 | 500 | 12.5 | +6.5 | 1.42 | 10.3 | 15/15 |

  Coinbase taker 0.45 bps (its dearest stable-pair tier), no transfer, margin 2 bps under Coinbase's touch, the hedge 30 s
  late. The 19 days run 10-10 → 10-29; a day with no fill is not counted. Halves: USDT-GBP £600 £0.33 → £1.14 a day, after the
  maker widened from 5 to 12 ticks on 10-13. So it depends on how wide the maker quotes. **USDT-GBP first:** its flow runs both
  ways; USDC-GBP's is mostly sales to us, so its inventory jams.
- **Fully automatable?**
  - Both legs and the fill detection, yes: Revolut X's REST orders and fills, Coinbase Advanced's IOC (both clients exist
    here). Revolut X has no websocket in its documentation, so fills are found by polling; the replay says once a minute is
    enough.
  - Transfers, no. Revolut X's API has no withdrawal endpoint, so moving coins is by hand in the app. Coinbase can withdraw by
    API only with a key allowed to transfer, which our rules forbid.
  - So run it without transfers. If a rebalance is ever wanted, Davies does it by hand in batches of £2,000 or more.
- **The 1,000 orders a day.** Revolut X's documentation: "The daily limit does not apply to sub-accounts"; "Each sub-account
  has … its own independent rate limits"; at most 300 active orders an account or sub-account. A, quoting one book at both
  sides, needs about 690–770 placements a day in the replay. **So A runs on a new sub-account of Davies' own Revolut X. A
  family member's account is not needed, and should not be used:** trading someone else's account is a terms and AML
  problem. LIVE already runs on sub-account `_2`.
- **Coinbase fee.** Coinbase's public product data flags USDT-GBP and USDC-GBP `fx_stablecoin: true` (minimum £0.72). The
  published stable-pair taker is 0.1 bps (bitbo's listing; Coinbase's 2022 stable-pair notice), and the reference allows up to
  0.45 bps by tier. The account's own tier was not read: `agents?action=cblive&mode=probe` reads it (`transaction_summary`),
  but calling it needs the cron bearer, and no earlier answer survives in `net._http_response`. At 0.1 bps every row gains
  0.35 bps.
- **Risks:**
  - The maker pulls or narrows. The cap at Coinbase's price keeps us from overpaying, and the replay's "through" queue (the
    maker matching us at once) costs a few fills, not the edge.
  - Coinbase unreachable. Its recorder showed 0 and 2 gaps over 120 s in 19 days (longest 120 s). Quote nothing on Revolut X
    while Coinbase is over 60 s stale; an unhedged fill is at most one Q.
  - De-peg. Both legs are the same coin, so only the coins held (half the capital) carry it, as LIVE's do.
  - No GBP/USD is needed at all.
  - Coinbase's minimum (£0.72, 0.01 coin) is no constraint.

**2. B (a 0.05 % rung inside the maker, on LIVE's rules) does not earn its build. Do not add it now.** Re-run with LIVE's own
813 fill prints taken out:
- On LIVE's book alone (USDT-GBP, £600) it adds −0.17 bps of capital a day under through-only fills and +0.26 under at-price
  fills (P(≤ 0) 0.60 and 0.30).
- Since the maker widened (10-13 →) it adds +0.14 and +0.80 (P 0.36 and 0.12).
- Its trips earn 5.6–5.9 bps each, but they take capital and fills from the outer rungs.
- Davies took the same idea out of rule D on 10-09 ("规则 D 最内层的档位基本不赚钱").
- If the maker stays at 12 ticks for a month, it can be asked again (§2).

**3. The maker earns little here: about £6–7 a day a book on the fills that are surely its own, at most about £19–21 a day
a book. We can take a slice of it, not beat it.**
- **Its fills.** Prints at a level that is one of its orders: 17.8 a day on USDC-GBP (£13,800 a day) and 20.9 on USDT-GBP
  (£15,100). Prints at levels it shares with others add 9.0 and 28.6 a day.
- **Its edge.** It captures 6.8 bps (USDC-GBP) and 5.2 bps (USDT-GBP) against fair, and 9.1–9.2 bps against Coinbase's mid.
  At the mid 30 minutes later it keeps 5.2 and 4.2 bps, so its adverse selection is only 1–1.6 bps.
- **Its money**:
  - **£7.1 and £6.3 a day** at the 30-minute mark on its sure fills (£18.8–21.0 a day with the shared ones added);
  - since 10-13, 6.0 bps realised on both books, against 2.6–4.1 before. Widening to 12 ticks paid it.
  - It buys more than it sells on USDC-GBP (379 against 208), so it must lay coins off elsewhere, probably at Coinbase's
    higher price: that would add the basis to its takings.
- **One tick inside it**, the same prints reach us first:
  - at £100 a fill we take 10–11 % of its pounds, at £500 34–37 %;
  - unhedged at the 30-minute mid that is 3.7 bps (USDC-GBP) and 2.4–2.6 bps (USDT-GBP), £0.4–1.7 a day a book;
  - hedged at Coinbase (A) it is 6–7 bps, because we cash the basis it cannot see in Revolut X's own book.
- **Where we have the edge**: Coinbase's price (the basis); no need to quote £3,700 orders; a fixed-width quote that does not
  react, so a price one tick better is always first.
- **Where it has the edge**: size, every hour, inventory it can lay off at scale, and likely the same 0 % maker fee.
- **"Beating" it** means taking 10–37 % of its flow at a tick better, with less adverse selection because Coinbase's price
  tells us when not to. Nothing in the data says it would leave. If it narrows to undercut us, that is ordinary competition,
  and the cap at Coinbase's price still holds.

**What should change in the live strategies now: nothing.** A goes to paper first (§1.4); B stays out.

## 1. A in depth (`scripts/xhedge.py` → `results/xhedge.json`)

### 1.1 What the replay does

- **Window and data:** 2026-10-10 02:00 → 10-29 00:00 UTC (18.9 days), both books.
  - Revolut X: the committed prints and five-level snapshots (fresh within 75 s of the recorder's last sight).
  - Coinbase: its touch every 30 s, from `cb_touch` at :00 (REVX-FAIR's copy) and `cb_book_levels`' best level at :30
    (`data/cb30_*.txt.gz`, `sql/cb_levels_touch.sql`). That is 53,750 and 53,218 reads.
- **The quote:** a bid at min(Revolut X best bid + 1 tick, its ask − 1 tick, Coinbase bid × (1 − m)), and the ask the
  same way round.
- **Fills, by queue model:**
  - "first": a print at or through our price fills us when we are inside the maker; a price we would only join fills only
    when traded through;
  - "half": half of each print;
  - "through": only prints strictly through (the maker matches us).
- **Hedge:** at the first Coinbase read at or after fill + d, paying the taker fee and one tick more on what exceeds
  Coinbase's best level.
- **Inventory:** capital C split four ways (Revolut X pounds and coins, Coinbase pounds and coins). A side quotes only while
  both its legs hold Q.
- **Transfers, when allowed:** at 00:00 UTC, coins move between the venues when the imbalance reaches the batch size.
  - Revolut X → Coinbase: £3 service (its published fee) plus network, £0.05 for USDC on Solana and £1 assumed for USDT.
  - Coinbase → Revolut X: USDC free; USDT 0.01 % (at most 20) plus £1.
  - Pounds go back by bank, free, a day later.
- **Revolut X orders:** a new placement every minute our wanted price moves, and after every fill.
- **Not counted:** the coins' value moving with GBP/USD (half the capital is coin, as with LIVE).

### 1.4 Build plan, paper first

1. **A real-time paper engine** (`agents/xhedge_paper.ts`, a row of `edge_calls`, every minute, keyless; its own
   `xh_paper_*` tables and lease; a TESTING row).
   - It reads only what is already recorded: `agent_book_levels`, `agent_quote_prints`, `cb_touch` and `cb_book_levels`.
   - Each minute it carries out §1.1 exactly: the quote, the fills by the prints, the hedge at the next Coinbase read, the
     four inventories.
   - A pin replays the committed window through the TypeScript engine and matches `xhedge.py` fill for fill.
2. **Live, only after a pass and on Davies' word**, with:
   - a NEW Revolut X sub-account and key (no daily order limit on sub-accounts);
   - a separate Coinbase portfolio and a trade-only key, so it never shares a book with "Stablecoin quotes USDC/GBP";
   - each minute: read Revolut X's active orders and fills, hedge each new fill on Coinbase by IOC at the touch (≤ 2 bps
     slippage), re-place the post-only quote when the wanted price moves;
   - guards: no Revolut X quote while Coinbase's data is over 60 s old; an unhedged-exposure cap of 2Q; the −1 % daily
     loss stop; the monitor's dead-man cancels on the sub-account; inventory floors;
   - no transfers.

**Pre-registration sketch (paper).**
- 28 days from its first full UTC day; USDT-GBP at C £1,200, Q £100, m 2 bps, no transfers; USDC-GBP as a second, separate
  arm.
- Fills and hedge as §1.1 ("first" inside, "through" when joined; Coinbase's next read, 0.45 bps).
- Pass: P&L > 0 and a day-bootstrap P(≤ 0) < 0.10, with at least 300 fills.
- Report: bps a fill, fills a day, the maker's width each day, minutes a side was blocked by inventory, Coinbase staleness,
  and Revolut X placements a day.
