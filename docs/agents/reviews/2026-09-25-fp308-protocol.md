## Where the fair value is published

The fair-value series is OKX `GET /api/v5/market/history-candles` for
`BTC-USDT` with `bar=1Dutc`, from `www.okx.com`. The public page is
`https://www.okx.com/docs-v5/en/#order-book-trading-market-data-get-candlesticks-history`.
The pull pages backward with `after`. Each row is
`[ts, o, h, l, c, vol, volCcy, volCcyQuote, confirm]`. `ts` is the candle open
in milliseconds. Every candle stored here starts at 00:00 UTC. Only a row with
`confirm` equal to `1` is stored, which is a candle that has closed. The close
is published when the candle ends, at `ts + 86400000`. A decision at a Binance
entry open uses an OKX close only when that end is strictly earlier than the
entry. This is the OKX spot book. It is not a Binance book and it is not the
OKX swap. Candles before 2022-10-29 and after 2023-12-31 are not stored.

The price being judged is the finished close of the contract being traded.
That close is not the fair value. The open of that contract is the fill and
is not an input. No book ticker is read. Binance spot, the coin-margined
book and the USDT book are not read as this fair value.

## The idea

**OKXCH.** Fair value is the OKX spot BTC-USDT daily close. The finished Binance spot close was strictly under that close, so the next Binance spot open is a price that finished cheap to OKX. Buy Binance spot for fourteen days. The other trade buys Binance spot for fourteen days on these same prices when that discount is absent. Same side. The edge is turned off. Not the opposite side. Not an unconditional hold. The entry open is not an input. One leg.

Zero basis points leaves the two sets the same length: 176 against 176. That is allowed. When the edge-off set is not shorter, every null draw is the full pool, so the house p95 equals the edge-off mean. The cutoff stays that p95. It is not moved to five basis points after a return. The rich side of this OKX premium is not a second rule, and a wider-basis rule is not shipped.
