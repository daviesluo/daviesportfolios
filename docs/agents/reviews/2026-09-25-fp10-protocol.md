# fp10 protocol: ETH's last bar, and DeFi TVL (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of these rules was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp9 are dead. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.
The lower tails of the variance gap and of dispersion are not opened here.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No price from 2024 onward is
pulled. The TVL file is cut at 2024-01-01 before it is stored.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the five closed rounds. Also not here: BTC's
own 4h trend, the ETH/BTC price ratio, a rule that holds ETH or the winner
coin, DVOL's level, the variance gap, basket dispersion, stablecoin supply,
and a dip defined by a down close. If ETH's bar fails, a second coin is not
the next candidate.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. A price or a TVL stamp on or after 2024-01-01 is refused, except
the 2024-01-01 BTC open, which may be an exit print. The null is fp5's: 200
draws, seed `20250925`, index 190. ETH-LEAD draws from every in-screen BTC
8h hold. TVL-UP draws from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at
least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above that rule's own
null.

## The ideas

1. **ETH-LEAD.** The signal is ETHUSDT only. At each 8h bar whose open is
   t, with t strictly before 2024-01-01, the return is that bar's close
   divided by the close of the bar opened exactly 8h earlier, minus 1. Both
   closes must be positive and both bars must exist. A missing bar is a
   skip. The return is known at t+8h, and that is its timestamp. It fires
   when the return is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
   percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[t+8h−90 days, t+8h)`,
   percentile `sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter BTCUSDT at t+8h. Exit
   at t+16h. BTC's own return is not an input. The lower tail is not scored.
   This is not the live 4h trend, and it does not hold ETH.
2. **TVL-UP.** DefiLlama `https://api.llama.fi/v2/historicalChainTvl`. Each
   row is `date` (unix seconds, midnight UTC) and `tvl`. Stored as
   milliseconds. Rows on or after 2024-01-01 are dropped before the file is
   written. On day D the change is tvl[D] / tvl[D−1 day] − 1. The two stamps
   must be exactly one day apart and both values positive. Anything else is
   a skip. It fires when the change is strictly above its own trailing-90-day
   90th percentile, at least 90 earlier days, same percentile formula. Enter
   the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold one day. The lower tail is not scored.
   Stablecoin supply was already tested and is not this series.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. Neighbouring quantiles, the
80th and the 95th of the same series, are a veto that can only reject. They
are not scored on this screen. 2023 is not part of that bar. If nothing
passes, these two rules are dead and the next search does not inherit their
cuts.
