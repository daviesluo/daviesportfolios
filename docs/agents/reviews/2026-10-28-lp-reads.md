kept the restart's stop basis (`state.lp.stop`), so conditions 1 and 3 were rebuilt from the records:

- the fills are every CONFIRMED row of `pm_lp_fills`;
- each token is marked at its YES book's mid in `pm_lp_minutes` (NO is 1 − that), at the latest minute at or before the
  instant, or at its payout once `pm_lp_settlements` shows it settled;
- the fees are 0: every fill since the restart carries `fee_rate_bps` 0.

Checked against today's holdings, the rebuild differs from the path's own `state.pnl.total` by $0.21, or 0.14 %.

Condition 3's 10-14 margin is under a dollar, so a different mark could tip it. That would not change the outcome.

The −$25 stop never tripped:
