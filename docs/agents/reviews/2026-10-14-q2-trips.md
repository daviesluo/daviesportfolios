## The answer in plain words

- **Every trip loses because the price keeps going the taker's way, for hours.** Our 16 lots, marked at the cleaned mid,
  lost −$16.38. Their parts:
  - the half-spread we earn at entry: +$5.87;
  - the first minute after the fill: −$9.12;
  - the drift while the lot is held: −$16.08;
  - the exit, against the mid at the exit minute: +$3.00.

  So the exits are not bad prices: they rest a tick inside the others' touch and fill above the mid. The losses come in
  two steps:
  - **The takers who hit us know something.** Even an exit at the mid one minute after the fill would have lost −$3.25.
  - **The lots are held a long time** (median 260 minutes, 10 of 16 over four hours) while the price walks away. The
    exit is re-quoted at the moving touch every minute, so it follows the price down.

  Averaging up to 2N cost −$4.19 (the 4 lots added while already holding). The three lots held through x2's pause lost
  −$2.10. Both are inside the −$16.38, not on top of it.
- **No rule priced here is positive with confidence at this size.**
  - On the raw engine, no rule beats Q2 as it runs now over the eight recorded days. Every paired difference is within
    noise.
  - Calibrated to live (rewards ×0.57; fills charged live's extra −0.92 ¢ a filled share), Q2 as it runs now is
    **+$0.38 a day [−1.73, +2.64]** on the record, and **−$12.45 [−20.92, −4.29]** over the live window, which is close
    to live's own −$13.50.
  - The best rule is to rest entries 4 ¢ from the adjusted mid: fewer fills and less reward. It is **+$1.78 a day
    [−0.14, +3.87]** calibrated on the record, and **+$0.33 [−3.77, +5.30]** on the live window.
  - Its rewards a filled share, calibrated, are 1.83 ¢ on the record but **0.77 ¢ on the live window**, under the 1.5 ¢
    bar.
  - It is also the best of 27 rules tried, so even that is flattered.
- **Verdict: stop live-prep for good.** That means close-only, letting the four held lots exit, with no restart.
  - I am about **80 % sure** that nothing in this family makes Polymarket's rewards pay for the fills at $20 a side in
    quiet books.
  - The rewards are about $2 a day (live: $2.26 in 26.6 hours, 10-14's at day 1's R). One informed taker costs $1–2.
  - If Davies still wants one last bounded test, Addendum 23 below is the only candidate. The stop's $9.00 of room caps
    what it can lose.

Over the longer window, the engine misses live on both sides:

- **Fills.** It loses −1.96 ¢ a filled share where live lost −2.88 ¢. Its fill rule takes our quote out of the book and
  meets the tape with no notion of who the taker is.
- **Rewards.** It overstates them 1.75× (×0.57). x2's pause trips in 1,222 selected minutes live against 200 in the
  engine, and those minutes carried 3.17 of its 10.21 of formula.

The calibration therefore charges both:

- rewards × 0.571;
- fills: the engine's own, plus −0.918 ¢ on every share the rule fills.

A rule that fills less is charged less, and a fill that wins keeps its sign. The ranges are the market-day bootstrap,
shifted by the charge. They do not carry the calibration's own uncertainty: 569 shares in 26.6 hours.

## 4. Verdict

**Stop live-prep for good.** Set it close-only so the held lots exit, and do not restart it.

- Two live days and the calibrated record agree on the magnitudes. Rewards are about $2 a day. The quiet books' takers
  cost more than the half-spread plus those rewards, and holds of hours let the price walk away.
- Every lever that makes a difference does so by trading less. At the limit, that is not trading.
- How sure: about 80 %. The leftover chance is that the record's raw engine (+$3.3 a day) is right and the 26.6 live
  hours were an unlucky draw. Even then, the live calibration of rewards (×0.57) leaves about +$1–2 a day at best, and
  the 1.5 ¢ bar is missed on the live window by every rule.

**If Davies wants one last bounded test instead, Addendum 23 (proposal only, not built):** BUY entries rest 4 ¢ from the
adjusted mid instead of a tick inside the touch, never better than RW's quote. Exits are unchanged.

- **Code.** In `supabase/functions/agents/pm_lp.ts`, `lpQuotesOn` (lines 255–278), after `q = quote(row, tick)`:
  - for the BUY intents only, `bYes = min(q.b, floorTick(q.m − 0.04, tick))` and
    `aYes = max(q.a, ceilTick(q.m + 0.04, tick))`, so a BUY of NO rests at `1 − aYes`;
  - every SELL of what is held keeps `q.b` / `q.a`;
  - the 4 ¢ becomes `PM_LP_SHAPE.entryDistCents = 4` (`LpShape`, lines 244–246), so `lpRulesOf` keeps the earlier
    shapes' tests;
  - no change in `pm_live.ts`.
- **Pins** in `supabase/functions/agents/pm_lp.test.ts`:
  1. `PM_LP_SHAPE.entryDistCents` is 4.
  2. On a book 0.20 / 0.40 (adjusted mid 0.30) with nothing held, BUY YES rests at 0.26 and BUY NO at 0.66. Today's code
     rests 0.21 and 0.61, so the pin fails on it.
  3. With YES held ≥ N, the SELL of YES stays at RW's ask (0.39).
  4. On a 0.30 / 0.34 book the BUY YES rests at 0.28, never better than RW's 0.31. On a 0.29 / 0.31 book nothing new

  5. Addendum 21's exit under the 4 ¢ touch is unchanged.
- **Bounded by the existing stop:** $9.00 of room. Read it at day 7 against the 14-day bar.

## 5. What this did not check
