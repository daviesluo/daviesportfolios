## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. This file adds none.
Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print
and is not filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of this same stop-entry payoff on every day whose high trades strictly above yesterday's high. The width filter is off in the null. The strategy is the subset of those breaks whose range is also strictly wider than each of the three prior days. Prices stop on 2023-12-31. No 2024 open is read. A later open is a bug. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net
above zero, and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**BRKHI.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, the high trades strictly above yesterday's high, and today's high minus today's low is strictly wider than each of the three UTC days before it. A missing prior day is not a signal and is not filled in. An equal range does not fire. A percentile is not used. This is not the high over yesterday's high as a ratio, entered the next day, and it is not a gap rule.

Buy yesterday's high. If the open is already strictly above that high, buy the open. Sell today's close. The fill is fp5's `net_return` from that entry price to the close. The trade is the break day itself.
