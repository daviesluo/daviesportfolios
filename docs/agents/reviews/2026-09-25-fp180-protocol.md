## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. This file adds none.
Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print
and is not filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of an open-to-close long on every in-screen day. The strategy is that session only after a close strictly above the open of a prior down day, so it is a subset.
The next day's open, through 2024-01-01, may finish a 2023 signal. It is not
a 2024 screen. A later open is a bug. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net
above zero, and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**REVCLOSE.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, the prior UTC day closed strictly under its open, and day D's close is strictly above that open. Both are known when day D closes. A close equal to that open does not fire. A prior up day does not fire. A percentile is not used. The prior high is not the buy price.

Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open and sell that day's close. The fill is fp5's `net_return`. The entry day's high, low and close are not an input to the decision to enter.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01 is scored. If
this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the signal
or the hold. This file does not arm a testing row.
