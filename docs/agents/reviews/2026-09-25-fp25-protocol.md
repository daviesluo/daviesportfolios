# fp25 protocol: coins held on exchanges (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp24 are dead, including LS-FADE and FEE-HIGH. None of their cuts
is reused, and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No stock stamp on or after
2024-01-01 is stored. The 2024-01-01 BTC daily open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the twenty closed rounds. Also not here:

* Exchange inflow, outflow, and their difference. fp8 scored the upper tail
  of net coins leaving exchanges. This rule does not read either flow. A
  large day's outflow that leaves the stock under its own 90th is not a
  signal. The lower tail of the stock, an unusually empty set of
  exchanges, is not scored.
* Total transaction fees, the mean fee, and fees as a share of miner
  revenue. FEE-HIGH stays closed. A cheap-fee day is not a candidate.
* Hash rate, block count, active-address counts, and transfer counts.
* Spot quote volume, trade count, and the price path: the share of up
  hours, the overnight gap, the close's place in the range, and the close
  versus VWAP. The spot file stores the open, because that is the fill.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**EXCH-BAL.** One series: Coin Metrics community `SplyExNtv` for `btc`,
daily. The signal day is the UTC date of `time` (the first ten characters,
`YYYY-MM-DD`). The value is the native units held in exchange addresses
that day. A non-positive value is missing. A duplicate stamp is missing.
A stamp on or after 2024-01-01 is refused.

It fires when the stock is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier positive prints in `[D−90 days, D)`,
percentile `sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily
open. Hold one day. The spot open on the signal day is not the entry.
A thin stock is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same stock are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
