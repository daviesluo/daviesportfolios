# Q2-AGE: should Q2 leave out markets younger than a few days?

Research only (2026-10-13, about 14:00–15:30 UTC). Q2 has been live since 13:25:32 UTC. Its rules are live-prep's
Addendums 19 and 20, and nothing of them is changed here. Every database read was a read-only `select`, and every
Polymarket read was keyless. Scripts and outputs are in `docs/agents/backtests/lpbest/`:

- scripts: `scripts/q2age.py`, `q2age_mech.py`, `q2age_13.py`;
- outputs: `results/q2age.txt`, `q2age_mech.txt`, `q2age_13.txt`;
- the manifest: `MANIFEST_q2age.json`.

LP-BEST's own files stay as they were frozen. Not blind: PMOPPS' age table was read first.

Davies, 2026-10-13, verbatim: "好的 继续深度检验和验证这两个" (good, keep testing and verifying these two in depth).

## The answer in plain words

- **PMOPPS saw something real, but smaller and different from what it looked like.** Through LP-BEST's engine, the one
  validated against live, Q2 *without* an age filter makes more money on the eight days:
  - +$3.61 a day at $316, against +$2.48 with a 3-day minimum;
  - the same on the days the rule was chosen on and on the days it was read on.
- **But young markets are where Q2's bad days come from.** With the 3-day filter:
  - the worst day goes from −$10.34 to −$2.40;
  - the daily swings shrink by 40 %.
- **The newest data agrees with the tail, not the mean.** On 10-13 00:00 → 12:59 UTC, the archive's hours after
  LP-BEST's span:
  - Q2 picked eight markets under six hours old;
  - two of them, an earnings question and a Rotten Tomatoes score, lost $17;
  - Q2 lost −$9.87 over the 13 hours, where the filtered rule made +$4.28.
- **Why.** Young quiet markets are not more toxic per share; their takers win about as much per share as in old
  markets. They are busier and pay far more reward, so Q2's ranking piles into them:
  - they are 7 % of the quiet books and 23 % of Q2's picks;
  - a busy market that jumps costs $5–$12 in a day.
- **What I recommend.**
  - **Do not adopt it now.** It fails the bar we set before looking: choose on TRAIN, read on TEST.
  - **Do not drop it either.** Watch it during Q2's 14-day test:
    - replay Q2 with the filter on the live window's pm-rec record;
    - split Q2's own live result by market age;
    - decide on day 14.
  - If a live day loses $10 or more and markets under three days old caused it, I would put the filter to you at once.
  - It would not have starved the budget: 31 to 85 quiet books a day stay eligible for 11 or 12 picks.

## 3. The mechanism (`results/q2age_mech.txt`)

Every quiet book of Q2's universe, stateless at RW's price, a market-day each:

| age at 00:00 | market-days | taker shares | takers' 60-min gain, ¢ a share | our fills, shares | our 60-min markout, ¢ a share | formula, $ a day | net, $ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| under 1 day | 33 | 698 | 2.04 | 94 | −1.38 | 4.86 | +0.74 |
| 1–3 days | 12 | 369 | 4.27 | 44 | −5.77 | 1.04 | −2.10 |
| 3–7 days | 38 | 174 | 1.74 | 20 | −0.60 | 0.79 | +0.21 |
| 7–30 days | 62 | 123 | 7.56 | 21 | −4.92 | 0.81 | −0.68 |
| 30 days + | 320 | 98 | 2.80 | 10 | −1.42 | 0.45 | +0.05 |

- **Not more toxic.**
  - In markets under a day old, takers gained 2.04 ¢ a share against 2.80 ¢ at 30 days or more.
  - Our markout was −1.38 ¢ a share against −1.42 ¢.
  - The 1–3-day row is the worst per share, but it is 12 market-days. So "early informed flow" is not what these quiet
    books show.
- **Bigger.** A day-old quiet market traded seven times the shares of an old one, filled us ten times as much, and its
  formula paid ten times as much.
- **Picked more.** RW's ranking rates them higher: they are 7.1 % of the quiet market-days and 23.4 % of Q2's
  market-days.
- **Q2's own young market-days paid well.** Under a day old, its 22 market-days made +$14.64 over the eight days:
  rewards +$14.50, fills −$0.90.
- **The cost is variance, not a bias.** One busy young market that jumps, an earnings line or a score, costs $5–$12 in
  a day. Both of Q2's bad days came mostly from such markets: 10-11 (−$10.34; −$3.39 with a 2-day minimum) and 10-13 (above).
- **PMOPPS' table, reconciled.** It called a book quiet on pm-rec's own tape, over the whole day table. Its −$123.81 on
  quiet books under 3 days old splits as:
  - −$117.58 in 1,214 market-days the data API never read, because they are outside Q2's candidate set: Q2's other
    rules already leave them out;
  - −$3.16 in 20 that the API calls busy;
  - −$3.07 in 92 that the API calls quiet.

  Among books Q2 can pick, the in-sample age effect is small. The engine's result above is the one to trust.

## 4. Verdict and proposal

- **Adopt now as an addendum: no.**
  - On the bar set before the data was read (choose on TRAIN, report TEST), no filter wins at $316 and at $400.
  - Over the eight days the filter costs about $1.1 a day [−$3.4, +$1.6].
- **Drop: no.**
  - It cuts the worst day by three quarters and the daily swings by 40 %.
  - On the 13 hours after LP-BEST's span it turns −$9.87 into +$4.28.
  - Q2's own live bar has a no-day-below −$15 condition and a −$25 stop, where tails matter more than a dollar of mean.
- **Test it alongside Q2's live window, without a new path.** Draft B as PMOPPS wrote it, a paper twin, would reopen a
  paper Reward quotes path. Those were all closed on 2026-10-13 (`0120`, on Davies' word), so the test below needs
  none. Draft B′:
  1. **Offline replay.** At day 7 (descriptive) and day 14, replay Q2 and Q2 + a 3-day minimum through this engine on
     the live window's pm-rec record (`q2age_13.py`, its day list set to the window). Same selection, same R, every
     number as here.
  2. **The live record split by age.** Q2's own live market-days, split into under and over 3 days by Gamma's
     `createdAt` at 00:00 (or at the pick, for refills), with each group's fills marked at the mids and the rewards
     paid.
  3. **Adopt by an addendum on day 14 only if both hold:**
     - the replayed Q2 + 3 days is at least the replayed Q2 over the window;
     - Q2's live market-days under 3 days old are negative in total.

     Otherwise drop it for good.
  4. **An early flag, Davies' call.** If a live day reaches −$10 or worse and markets under 3 days old account for most
     of it, the main session puts the filter to Davies that day, with the day's split. Nothing changes without his word.
- **If it is adopted, the change is small.**
  - One more candidate rule: `createdAt` at least 3 days before the selection's 00:00 (or the refill's minute), read
    from Gamma, which the selection already reads for every candidate. A market without one is not taken.
  - 2 and 3 days read the same on the 13 hours and nearly so on the eight days; 3 is the round number PMOPPS
    pre-stated.

## Not checked

- **The market's age, not the reward programme's.** `createdAt` is Gamma's market creation. A programme that starts on
  an old market is not "new" here.
- **Eight days and 13 hours.** The second span is a partial day whose fills are marked at 12:59, and no market-day
  bootstrap over 11 market-days can say much.
- **R in young pools.** The engine pays young and old pools at the one calibrated R. If Polymarket scores a young,
  busy pool differently, that changes the mean, not the variance.
- **The live record.** Q2 has been live for about two hours, so nothing of it is read here beyond its picks' ages.
