out by the live executor's code on a simulated Revolut X account, £600, £50 a rung, one governed key. Only the exit price

## 2. Why

The review's F3: an exit at fair sits inside the resident maker's spread and, in the twins, fills when the market trades
at that maker's far quote, usually two or more ticks through it. On the stored prints one tick further would still have
filled 76 of 77 of `p50`'s maker-exit trips (+£0.45, +8.6 % over its record) with a median delay of 0 minutes; 67 of 67 of
`pr5`'s; 335 of 336 of `d`'s. That indicator models neither the exit's re-price with fair nor depth shared with other
orders, so only running it can say.

## 3. Window, reading and bar

- **Window**: round trips **opened** (their entry's fill) from **2026-10-12 00:00 UTC** (the first Monday after the build
  lands) to **2026-11-09 00:00 UTC**, four weeks, 20 weekdays, in both arms. A trip belongs to the UTC day of its entry.
  `p50` must keep running through 2026-11-09 and until its window's trips close: removing it before then voids the test.
- **Reading**: on or after **2026-11-11 00:00 UTC** (the window's end + 2 days), never before `p50`'s own readout of
  2026-10-21, by a script committed before 2026-11-09 00:00 UTC, from `agent_quote_twin_p50x1_*` and
  `agent_quote_twin_p50_*`, trips cut and booked by the page's own `liveRungs` / `liveRungTrips` (`agents/index.ts`).
  Trips still open at the reading are reported, not scored.
- **Bar, all three:**
  - **B1**: at least 60 closed trips opened in the window in each arm.
  - **B2**: `p50x1`'s realised P&L minus `p50`'s, over trips opened in the window, above zero.
  - **B3**: a day-block bootstrap of the 20 weekdays' differences (each weekday: `p50x1`'s realised over trips opened that
    day minus `p50`'s; a day with none is 0), Python `random.Random(20261012)`, 10,000 resamples of 20 days with
    replacement: the 5th percentile of the resampled sums above zero. Weekend days count in B1 and B2 and not in B3.
- **Descriptive beside it**: trips a side, exits filled at their first price, minutes from exit placed to filled, 24-hour
  stops, exit mark-outs, and how many buy-backs were sized to the venue's minimum (F1) in each arm.
- **A pass** goes to Davies as a candidate for LIVE's exit, at £10 or the size he chooses, with a pre-registration of its
  own; nothing moves on LIVE without his word. **A fail** leaves LIVE's exit at fair. Either way the twin stops at the
  reading unless Davies says otherwise.
- **Until the reading, health only**, daily: its `_sim.last_error`, `paperCheck.mismatches` equal to `p50`'s (46, all
  before 2026-09-24 18:13), its mode (catch-up until it reaches PR5's call, then forward), its state's `updated_at`
  against PR5's call, the call's beats, no `ops_errors` `agents.quotes_twins`. No figure of either arm is read for the
  test before the reading (there is no no-peek rule since 2026-10-04; the reading says what was read).

variants, and rule D's twin back to variant-3). Migration `0109` deletes this twin's spec row, drops its six tables and

**Why.** The F3 replay (`docs/agents/backtests/scq_f3/`, its `MANIFEST.json` findings) ran the twins' own code over
2026-09-23 15:09 → 10-09 16:00 UTC, its baseline equal to the twins' records to the penny. One tick beyond fair wins only
under the twins' through-only fill rule (`p50` +£0.244, +4.6 %, its second half about zero) and loses when a print at the
exit's price fills it (`p50` −£0.198; `pr5` +£0.652 and −£0.702); LIVE filled 22 of its 44 maker exits with no print
through them, so the at-price rule is the nearer one. This twin, on the through-only simulator, could see only the side
that favours it, so §4's bar would not have read what it was meant to.

**What it ran.** Its offset never started: from `0108` to `0109` it loaded its backfill and caught up minute by minute,
reaching 2026-10-07 16:46 UTC (production, read-only, 2026-10-09 18:24 UTC: 3,540 orders, 270 events), every turn
`p50`'s. Nothing of it is lost by the drop.

**What stays.** The rule extension `exitOffset` (`TWIN_RULES.exitOffset` in `quotes_twin.ts`,
`QuoteLiveInstance.exitOffset` in `quotes_live.ts`) and their tests stay, named by no row, so no twin runs it: taking it
out would change `quotes_twin.ts`'s hash, which the twins' and `p50`'s pre-registrations pin, a further deviation in each
for code nothing reaches. `p50x1.json.gz` and `src/p50x1_twin.test.js` left the repository with the twin (both at commit
d0c6f823); §5's hashes are kept as they were frozen and no longer pinned by a test.
