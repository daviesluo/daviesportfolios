# fp333 protocol: PEPRM (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The number this file
scores is the change in the perpetual premium, in basis points, between
two daily opens. The move in the coin over that day is not the result.
The funding cash is not added.

Who is wrong: the buyer of `1000PEPEUSDT` pays more, per coin, than the
`PEPEUSDT` open. Binance quotes that perpetual as one thousand PEPE, so
the coin price is the perpetual price divided by 1,000
(`https://bitcoinmargin.com/pepe-usdt`, reviewed 7 Sept 2026: one
`1000PEPEUSDT` contract covers 1,000 PEPE). A published note on the
perpetual premium says the premium is the gap between the perpetual and
the spot, and the funding payment is the mechanism written to pull that
gap back (`https://perpfinder.com/tools/premium`). The funding print is
not this rule's result. On this name the funding print never reached 25
basis points in 2023, so it cannot clear a forty-basis-point round trip,
and it is not substituted for the premium.

The premium is `(perpetual / 1000 − spot) / spot`, both legs this venue's
own prices, in basis points. At the entry it is the two opens. At the
exit it is the next day's two opens. Gross is that difference divided by
10,000. Four fills at ten basis points each come off, so forty basis
points come off. What remains is the premium change after that round trip.
The hold is one day because the next open is the first open at which the
premium can be read again. A longer hold would mix later premiums into
this one.

The signal is the previous day's closes. It has finished before the entry
open. The entry open is not in the signal. High and low are not stored.
Another venue's close is not an input. The premium index is not an input.
The mark price is not an input. The rest of the pre-specified basket does
not clear fifty basis points on thirty days, and those coins are not added.
FTT's perpetual close is the constant 1.59 and is not a book. The side on
which the perpetual is cheap is the same sentence with the sign changed
and is not a second rule.

The count stays 30. A mean that is negative, or that clears the edge-off
trade by 20 bps or less, is not taken out of sample. Later years are scored
only when the mean net is positive and strictly more than 20 bps above the
edge-off trade, and only in a later file. LS-FADE's 60-trade reproduction,
the control, doubled costs, and the rule that no month is above 40% of the
profit are not relaxed. A Binance open is not a Revolut X fill. This screen
is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an adoption and is
not a testing row. 2023 is spent once this screen runs. Nothing here changes
a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. Public reads only. One hundred
dollars stands on the package, so the dollar result is one hundred times
the net of the premium change. Each signal day is one such package.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a fill. The package is short the perpetual and long the
spot, four fills, forty basis points. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)`
UTC. The exit is the next daily open. The last stored day has no next open
and is neither trade. The listing day has no previous close and is neither
trade. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of the same
package when the previous close premium is below 50 basis points, including
a negative premium. When that set has more fills than this rule, the cutoff
is the house p95 of samples of this rule's count. It is not replaced by
that set's own mean. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero,
and mean net above that null.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE stays as written: both later sub-windows
positive, above a fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30
trades, no month above 40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more
than 4% a year on the $100 it locks. The 60-trade reproduction of LS-FADE
is not relaxed. A pass that clears the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less
stays inside 2023. If this rule fails, it is dead. This file does not arm
a testing row.

## The idea

**PEPRM.** The previous close premium is at least 50 basis points. Short
the perpetual and long the spot until the next daily open. The other trade
is the same package when the previous close premium is below 50 basis
points. Same side. The edge is turned off.

Thresholds counted before this freeze, with no profit computed, on the
239 fillable days. The fee is forty basis points, so a line below that
fee is not a candidate. The shipped line is the smallest grid point above
that fee at which the rich set has at least 30 days and is strictly
shorter than the edge-off set.

has 45 days. It is the same premium with the sign changed and is not scored.
