# fp317 protocol: USOFR (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The price edge is
stated below, before the count. Davies, 2026-09-25: another exchange's spot
or futures close is not the fair value. Settled funding, a published implied
volatility, or a macro series may be used only when it was already published
at the decision and it is not inside the daily bar being traded. The fill is
that contract's own open. A rule that cannot name the fair value the price
paid is cheap against is not run. Eight rules use two sources. A book ticker
is not read. A print that was not yet published at the decision is not used.

The signal is the latest settled funding rate whose `fundingTime` is strictly
earlier than the daily open. The open is not an input. The daily close is not
an input. A missing open is not taken from another book. The position is one
leg. It is not a two-leg spread. The count stays 30. A mean that clears the
edge-off trade by 20 bps or less is not taken out of sample. Later years are
scored only when the three screen bars pass and the mean net is strictly more
than 20 bps above the edge-off trade, and only in a later file. LS-FADE's
60-trade reproduction, the control, doubled costs, and the rule that no month
is above 40% of the profit are not relaxed. A Binance daily open is not a
Revolut X fill.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption and is not a testing row. 2023 is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. Public
reads only. Ten basis points a side. One hundred dollars stands on the
one leg, so the dollar result is one hundred times that leg's net.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on the one leg. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC.
An exit may be the 2024-01-01 open when the hold reaches that day. That
open is not an entry and not a signal. A day with no print is not filled
in, and it is not borrowed from another contract. The null is 200 draws,
seed `20250925`, index 190, of the edge-off trade named below. When that
trade has more fills than this rule, the cutoff is the house p95 of samples

## Where the series is published

Settled funding is `GET /fapi/v1/fundingRate` on `https://www.binance.com`
for `BTCUSDT`. `fundingTime` is the settlement time and `fundingRate`
is the rate. `https://fapi.binance.com` and `https://dapi.binance.com` return
451 from the network this was read on, so they are not the host. The public
description of the formula is Binance's funding-rate note,
`https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/introduction-to-binance-futures-funding-rates-360033525031`.
The monthly archive
`https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/monthly/fundingRate/BTCUSDT/`
was compared with this history before any return. On the USD-M books the
archive and the history endpoint match on every stamp and every rate, 1188
rows. On the coin-margined books the monthly archive has 1149 rows. The 39
missing stamps are the last day of each month. The history endpoint has them,
and the overlapping rates match. This rule stores the history endpoint so
those settlements are present. A stamp that is not strictly earlier than the
entry is not used. The row stamped on the entry hour, including a stamp a
few milliseconds after the hour, is not used.

SOFR is `GET /api/rates/secured/sofr/search.json` with `type=rate`, from `https://markets.newyorkfed.org/api/rates/secured/sofr/search.json?startDate=2022-12-01&endDate=2024-02-01&type=rate`. The public page is `https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/sofr`. The rate for a business day is published the next US government-securities business morning, around 8:00 a.m. New York time, and may be revised until about 2:30 p.m. New York time. This screen does not treat `effectiveDate` as the publication time. Publication is 13:00 UTC on the next `effectiveDate` in the series, which is that next business morning. 13:00 UTC is after both 8:00 a.m. Eastern and the same-day revision window, and it does not move a midnight decision relative to 12:00 UTC. A Friday print whose next effective date is Tuesday is not available on Monday. A print whose publication is 2024-01-02 is not stored. A missing rate is neither side.

The daily open of `BTCUSDT` is the fill. It is read from the vision
daily kline, open only. The close, high and low are not stored. No book
ticker is read.

## The idea

**USOFR.** Fair value is the price index grossed up by the already-published SOFR carry. The last settled BTCUSDT funding rate is strictly below that eight-hour SOFR rate, so the contract finished cheap to the cash-and-carry. Buy the USDT book for two days. The other trade buys it for two days when the settled rate is not below that SOFR rate. The fair value is the price index grossed up by the already-published SOFR carry. SOFR is an actual/360 overnight rate. The eight-hour equivalent is `percent / 100 * (8 / 24) / 360`. The price paid is this contract's own later open. It is cheap when the last settled funding is strictly below that eight-hour rate. Funding cash is not added to the result. The comparison is the price versus the carry, not a funding payment. Same side. The edge is turned off. Not the opposite side. Not an unconditional hold. The entry open is not an input. One leg.

Holds from two days upward were counted before this freeze. Two days is the
shortest hold that is not one day, and it is the first line at which this
rule has at least 30 spans and the edge-off set is at least as long. No
longer hold was substituted after a count. The constant 0.01% interest rate
was not used as a second threshold.

A count of spans, taken before this freeze, with no profit computed: the rule
has 126 spans and the edge-off trade has 238. Fillable
days were 364 on the traded book.

Option end-of-hour files were not used. The daily file is an archive of a
finished day, so it is not a same-night print. Lagged by a full extra day,
with a real traded open, no BTC or ETH call or put reached 30 spans. That
clock was not pulled forward to manufacture a pass.
