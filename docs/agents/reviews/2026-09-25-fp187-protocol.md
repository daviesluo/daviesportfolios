## The idea

**SPOTIDX.** On day D, strictly before 2023-12-31, the BTCUSDT spot close is strictly under the BTCUSDT index close. Both closes are known when that day closes. An equal close does not fire. A missing index day is not a print and is not filled in. This is not the premium index and it is not the perpetual. The high and the low are not read. A percentile is not used.

Enter the next BTCUSDT spot open and sell that day's close. The fill is fp5's `net_return`. The entry day's high, low and close are not an input to the decision to enter.
