# PR5: PR3's quotes on nine months of prints PR3 never saw (2026-09-23)

Davies asked why PR3 was tested on 13 days, and for a longer, credible test: "叫一个sub-agent做更久的可信的测试可以吗？如果还能通过验证的话就上paper testing".
PR3 had 13.25 out-of-sample days because it read Revolut X's 1-minute candles, which the venue keeps for 28 days.
The venue also serves its whole PUBLIC trade history without a key (`GET /api/1.0/public/trades/all?symbol=&start_date=&end_date=&limit=100&cursor=`,
a window of at most one day, 100 prints a page, one request a second), which the first program missed. A research agent pulled
every UK print of USDC/GBP and USDT/GBP since each book opened and re-tested PR3's rule, unchanged, on the nine months before PR3's
data began — data PR3's distances were never chosen on. Reference §3.27 is the summary; the paper test it led to is §4 item 31.

## What was re-computed before any of this was believed

* **Order of work.** The pre-registration (`2026-09-23-pr5-prereg.md`, sha256 `d4785087…`, byte for byte) was frozen at
  14:38:15Z; the first result on the primary window was written at 14:38:56Z. One dry run preceded the freeze, and it covered
  only 2026-08-26 → 09-09, PR3's own in-sample half, already seen (52 trips): the primary window was not touched. The
  simulator and its config hash the same now as in the manifest taken at the freeze.
* **Determinism.** Two re-runs of the frozen simulator gave the agent's JSON byte for byte (`ed87659b…`), and so did the copy
  committed here reading the inputs committed here (`docs/agents/scripts/pr5/pr5_sim.py`; the only change is where it reads from).
* **Fills are prints.** 30 trips drawn at random from the 8,192: every entry has a print strictly through the quote at its
  `fill_ts`, at or after the order went live, and every maker exit a print through the exit price in its exit minute. The
  44 taker exits the report names are there.
* **The regime change is in the tape.** Independently, weekly medians of the gap between a BUY print and a SELL print within
  60 s: USDT/GBP 9–24 bps in weeks 30–34 of 2026 (26 bps over weeks 2–33), then 2.7–5.4 bps from week 35 (2026-08-24) on;
  USDC/GBP 13–31 bps, then 2–8. The pass was earned in the wide market.
* **Not carried by dust.** A second research program the same day found a bot printing $0.10 trades at the UK touch. In PR5 the
  604 trips under $1 made $0.37 of the $707.90; trips of $10 or more made $693.90 on 5,806 trips. In the dry-run period after
  the change, 48 of 52 trips were $10 or more and made all of its P&L. The rule's size cap (10 % of the minute's volume) is
  what keeps a dust print from making a large fill.

### What the data forced (all written into the pre-registration)

- Fills: a bid fills on a print strictly below its price, an ask strictly above, at or after the minute the order goes live.
- Size: at most $100 and at most 10 % of the minute's printed volume.
- Post-only: an order is refused when the last print before it goes live is through its price, or sits at its price on the
  crossing side; it is re-placed, as one more order, once the tape shows the market has moved back.
- The taker stop closes at the last print.
- Fair value: the median of the USD books' UK hourly closes; it equals PR3's own fair exactly in 94.7 % and 86.8 % of minutes
  (print-based alternatives matched 32–43 %).
- Interbank rate: Exness's public tick archive, chosen before any P&L.

### Data checks

- The tape: USDC-GBP 15,466 prints (2025-10-01 → 09-22), USDT-GBP 41,478 (2025-12-10 → 09-22), all region UK; no day failed, no
  row outside its request window, no id twice with different fields (2,824 duplicate rows from two pullers fetching the same 20
  days, removed by id), timestamps in milliseconds; every Revolut X request waited for the shared 1.1-second lock.
- Completeness against the venue's UK candles: hourly volume matches in 7,201 of 7,207 hours (USDC/GBP) and 6,713 of 6,743
  (USDT/GBP), counting a print from the first 1,000 ms of a minute in the previous minute's candle as the candle builder does;
  in 35 of the 36 misses the tape holds MORE than the candle (0.03 % and 0.6 % of volume); in one hour the candle holds 1,001 USDT
  more than the prints. Minute by minute against PR3's committed candles: 40,314 and 40,312 of 40,318 minutes match.
- USD books: USDT-USD 22,856 prints over 272 days, all UK; USDC-USD 1,427 of 13,304 prints UK (10.7 %) over the 30 days pulled.
  The rule reads neither.
- FX: Exness against Yahoo's hourly GBPUSD=X over 5,026 hours median −0.29 bps, median |dev| 0.43, p95 1.66; against FXCM's
  1-minute archive median |dev| 0.11 bps; against PR3's Yahoo 1-minute file median |dev| 0.37. Kraken rejected (−1.15 bps under
  Yahoo hourly, trades at weekends), FXCM (no files for 2026-W18 → W31), Dukascopy (refused this machine).
- Too-good checks: on PR3's period the print-based rule makes +$11.67 on 102 trips (PR3's candle version +$9.13 on 100), every
  one of those fills and exits checked against the raw prints; no look-ahead (the one in-minute input is the size cap, PR3's
  convention); the median filling print was 8.1 bps through the quote; wicks over 50 bps gave 9 trips and $2.20; the top 10 %
  of trips made 38 % of P&L; filling only on the opposite aggressor side gives +$694.65, and without the post-only refusal the rule
  makes +$793.57.

### What surprised the agent

1. The step in late August: the first program read "the gap shrank all year" from hourly candles; in the prints it is a sudden
   drop in the week PR3's data began.
2. Both books traded before their candles begin: USDC/GBP has prints on 2025-01-15 and every day from 2025-10-01, USDT/GBP from at
   least 2025-06; candle volume is zero until 2025-11-26 16:00 and 2025-12-16. That earlier tape was not tested.
3. The candles are not a reliable count: they lag about a second at minute boundaries, drop a few prints, and the daily candles
   contradict the hourly ones on 16 days.
