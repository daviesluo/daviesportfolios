# fp57 protocol: the change in base volume (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp53 are dead, including LS-FADE, BODY and its out-of-sample
result, BAL-CHG, IMPACT and its out-of-sample result, PEAK, STREAK,
OPEN-DRIVE, AVG-CHG and its out-of-sample result, and the screens fp30
through fp53. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.
IMPACT's 80th and 95th are not candidates. BODY's 80th is not a
candidate. PEAK's close under the prior 30-day high, and its 80th, are not
candidates. STREAK's, OPEN-DRIVE's and AVG-CHG's 80th and 95th are not
candidates. AVG-CHG's 95th was positive and does not save it. The level of
the address count is not a candidate, and neither is BAL-CHG's 80th.
UP-STEP beat its null on 25 trades. The count stays 30, and those later
years were not pulled. A rule rejected without a scored run does not count.
The count of 30 is not lowered. The window is not shrunk.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No daily base volume from 2024 onward is stored. The 2024-01-01 base volume is stored as 0. A later open is a bug.

## Not this search

* The change in quote volume. QVOL-CHG stays closed. Quote volume is not an input. Base volume can rise while quote volume does not, because the price is not in this ratio.
* The change in quote per trade. AVG-CHG stays closed, including its later years. Its 95th was positive and is not a candidate. The trade count is not an input.
* The change in the trade count, and the level of either volume. TRD-CHG and AMIHUD stay closed. A day whose base volume equals yesterday's scores 0 however large it is.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**BASE-CHG.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, divide that day's base volume by the previous day's base volume and subtract 1. Yesterday is exactly one day earlier. Both volumes must be positive. A day that did not grow stays in the history and is not a signal. The quote volume and the trade count are not read. A missing bar is a skip.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile and strictly above 0, at least 90 earlier prints in
`[D−90 days, D)`, percentile `sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next
BTCUSDT daily open. Hold one day. A day whose base volume did not rise is
not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto
that can only reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored
on this screen.
