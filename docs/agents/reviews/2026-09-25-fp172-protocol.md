## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. This file adds none.
Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print
and is not filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of that same stop on every in-screen day, down day or not. The level is always the prior day's low. The strategy is that stop only after a day that closed strictly under its open, so it is a subset. Prices through 2024-01-01 may finish a 2023 signal. They are not a 2024 screen. A later open is a bug.
A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**STOPLOW.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, the close is strictly under the open. That is known when day D closes. Day D's low is known then too. A close equal to the open does not fire. A percentile is not used. An up day does not fire.

Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. The stop level is day D's low, known before that open. If the open is already at or under the level, the exit is the open. If the low later trades at or under the level, the exit is the level. Otherwise the exit is the close. The entry day's high is not read. The entry day's low and close are the stop's fill. They are not the reason to enter. The fill is fp5's `net_return` on the open and that exit price.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01 is scored. If
this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the hold or
the cut.
