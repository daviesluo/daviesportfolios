# fp16 protocol: intraday return autocorrelation (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp15 are dead, including LS-FADE and RV-RATIO. None of their cuts
is reused, and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No bar opened on or after
2024-01-01 is pulled, except the 2024-01-01 BTC daily open, which may be an
exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the eleven closed rounds. Also not here:

* The size of the day's high-low range, either tail. This rule does not
  read high or low.
* The ratio of 7-day to 30-day realized vol, either tail.
* DVOL, DVOL minus realized vol, and cross-sectional dispersion or skew.
* A Donchian trend, a pullback, or a hold shorter than one day. The hours
  are how the signal is built. The position is the next daily bar.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**AC-HOUR.** BTCUSDT 1h bars. On day D, a midnight strictly before
2024-01-01, take the 24 bars whose opens are D, D+1h, …, D+23h. Each
return is that bar's close divided by its own open, minus 1. Both must be
positive. If any of the 24 opens is missing, or any open is on or after
2024-01-01, the day is not a print.

The statistic is the lag-1 correlation of those 24 returns: the population
correlation of return[0..22] with return[1..23]. The covariance and both
variances divide by 23, not by 22. If either variance is zero, the day is
not a print. The correlation is known when the 23:00 bar closes, and its
timestamp is D.

It fires when the correlation is strictly above its own trailing-90-day
90th percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. The negative correlation — a day that reversed every hour — is
not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same correlation are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the lag or the cut.
