# fp43 protocol: quote volume in up hours (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp37 are dead, including LS-FADE, BODY and its out-of-sample
result, BAL-CHG, IMPACT and its out-of-sample result, PEAK, and the eight
screens fp30 through fp37. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is
flipped. IMPACT's 80th and 95th are not candidates. BODY's 80th is not a
candidate. PEAK's close under the prior 30-day high, and its 80th, are not
candidates. The level of the address count is not a candidate, and neither
is BAL-CHG's 80th. TURN's count stays 30. The unscored side of TURN,

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No hourly bar on or after 2024-01-01 is stored. An hourly bar stores the open, the close and quote volume. The 2024-01-01 BTC daily open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

* The share of hours that closed up. UP-SHARE stays closed. One up hour that
  holds the day's volume scores near 1 here and 1/24 there.
* Quote volume lined up with the absolute hourly move. IMPACT stays closed,
  including the later years. Its 80th and its 95th are not candidates. This
  rule splits volume by the sign of the hour, not by the size of the move.
* The taker share. Its lower tail stays closed and is not flipped.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**UP-VOL.** BTCUSDT hourly opens, closes and quote volume. A day needs all
24 hours. Each open and close must be positive, and quote volume must not be
negative. The statistic is the sum of quote volume in hours whose close is
strictly above the open, divided by the sum of quote volume. The day's quote
sum must be positive. A flat hour adds to the denominator only. Scaling
every hour's quote volume by the same positive constant leaves the statistic
unchanged. A missing hour is a skip. It is not filled. One known hole,
2023-03-24 13:00 UTC, drops that day. The statistic is known when the day
closes, and its timestamp is that day.

It fires when the share is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto
that can only reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored
on this screen.
