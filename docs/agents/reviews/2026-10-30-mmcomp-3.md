## 1. Method

- **Data:** REVX-FAIR's stored record (`fair_price/data/`): PR5's minute record, every Revolut X UK print, the book snapshots
  (five levels a side, stored when they change, each with the last time the recorder saw it unchanged), and Coinbase's touch
  (from 10-10, merged with MMCOMP-2's 30-second touch in `mmcomp/data/`). Window 2026-09-26 -> 10-29 00:00 UTC, 33 days, split at
  10-13 (17 + 16 days).
- **LIVE's own prints taken out:** 813 (USDT-GBP 607, USDC-GBP 206), matched by the first 8 hex characters of each id (unique in
  the file; all 813 found).
- **The engine:** PR5-OPT's (`pr5_opt/scripts/sim.py`, imported unchanged). `run2` in `inside.py` is a copy of its `run` with
  hooks. With no hook on, it gives the same trips as `sim.run`: 226 of 226 on LIVE's rules, 165 of 165 on RX USDC/GBP's
  (`engine_check`, asserted before anything else runs).
- **Today's rules:** LIVE = USDT-GBP alone, £100 on each 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 % rung a side (its weights since 10-29 22:30). RX
  USDC/GBP = USDC-GBP alone, £30 on each 0.1 / 0.2 % rung a side, the 0.3 % rungs quoting nothing. Both: exits a tick beyond
  fair, a rung held from £1, the 24-hour stop, the 0.05 % re-price, the de-peg guard. Every variant keeps the capital.
- **The maker's order** in a snapshot: the best level of 4,999.5-5,010.5 coins in one order, or a level of several orders
  holding 4,999.5-6,000 coins. Read at each turn from the last snapshot stamped at or before it and seen within 75 s; when it
  is not visible, no inside order rests.
- **The inside order:** one tick better than the maker's (bid +1 tick, ask -1), only if that is at least k bps from fair and
  does not cross the touch. The wide-only version also needs the maker's bid and ask 10 ticks or more apart. It uses the pounds
  of the outermost rung (LIVE 0.3 %, RX USDC/GBP 0.2 %): that rung quotes no entry while the maker is wide or while the inside
  order rests or holds, and the inside order rests nothing while that rung holds. So the capital out never passes the
  strategy's (`over_capital_share` 0 on every run).
- **Fills:** 'at' (LIVE's rule: through the price, or at it with the taker on the other side, our order first at its price)
  and 'through' (only prints through the price).
- **Statistics:** daily P&L by exit day in bps of capital, paired by UTC day against today's rules, circular 3-day block
  bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261030; 90 % band and P(≤ 0) (`inner_rung2.py`'s convention). Annualised as bps a day ×
  365.

What each says:
- **a. Capturing the full spread loses.** An exit as far out as the entry waits for a move the other way that rarely comes: trips
  fall from 6.7 to 2.9 a day and 37 positions end at the 24-hour taker stop. LIVE's exit a tick beyond fair is what turns its
  wide entries into money; the maker can wait because it has inventory on both sides and does not need each position closed.
- **b. Today's distances are right.** Closer than 0.1 % earns less a trip than it gains in trips; a single order at the maker
  (inside, joining or behind it) earns 5-7 bps a trip and loses to the ladder by 4-8 bps of capital a day.
- **c. Skew is noise at our size.** At most 1 bp, and only on one fill rule.
- **d. Faster re-pricing buys nothing** and needs about 1,400 placements a day, over LIVE's governor (entries withdrawn at 900
  POSTs a UTC day) on 23 of 33 days.
- **e. One big order is no better than three rungs**, and is worse before 10-13.
- **f. Pulling a side on a Coinbase move costs**, as the Coinbase nudge did (`reviews/2026-10-29-rx-usdc-live.md`). A 2-3 bp move
  in 60 s almost never happens on these books, so those rows barely act.

## 4. Inventory, the order cap, the loss stop and the share of the maker's flow

- **Inventory:** under today's rules every bid rung of LIVE is holding (that side full, nothing left to quote) on 7.5 % of
  turns and every ask rung on 5.3 %. With the wide-only inside order neither side was ever full, on either book. The £1
  holding line and the capital cap are unchanged; the capital out never passed £600 / £120.
- **The order cap:** both accounts are Revolut X sub-accounts (LIVE on key `_2`; RX USDC/GBP on the retired trend loop's key,
  created under Revolut X's Sub-accounts feature, `reference.md` §5 item 2). Revolut X's documentation says the daily order
  limit does not apply to sub-accounts (quoted in MMCOMP-2's sources). LIVE's own governor still withdraws entries at 900 POSTs
  a UTC day. The wide-only inside order needs about 300 placements a day on LIVE (it follows the maker's every move); the
  busiest day was 925 ('at') / 957 ('through'), the only day over 900. On RX USDC/GBP: 222 a day, at most 779.
- **The 1 % daily loss stop** is never near: the worst replayed day is -5.4 bps of capital on LIVE's book and -12.9 on RX
  USDC/GBP's, against -100.
- **Share of the maker's flow:** the inside order's entries come to 1.7 % ('at') / 1.0 % ('through') of the maker's own fills
  in pounds on USDT-GBP, 0.4 % / 0.3 % on USDC-GBP. The order is £100 (or £30), rests only while the maker is wide, and stays
  out while its rung holds. MMCOMP-2's 10-37 % was a quote resting inside the maker all the time at £100-500 a fill.
- **k:** 2 and 4 bps from fair give almost the same result (+3.22 and +2.88 on LIVE, 'at'). In the wide regime one tick inside
  the maker is about 6.7 bps from the middle, so k only matters at the edges; 6 bps begins to cut fills. 2 bps would be the
  pick.

## 5. What the replay cannot model

- **The maker answering us.** A quote one tick in front of it takes flow it used to get. It could step in front again, narrow,
  or leave. Its width changed on 10-13 and on 10-28 and it has been gone since 10-29 21:16, all for reasons the tape does not
  give. Nothing here can say how it would react, and that is the largest unknown.
- **Takers answering us.** The replay gives us the prints that happened. A better price could draw more flow, or a taker who
  only wanted the maker's 5,000 coins might not trade our £100 the same way.
- **The book between snapshots.** Snapshots are stored when the book changes, read about once a minute. The maker can move in
  between; we would rest a tick inside a price it has left. Its own fills move its order too (a part-filled order is under
  4,999.5 coins and is not recognised until it is topped up).
- **Our own orders in the snapshots.** LIVE's resting orders are in the recorded touch, so the 'do not cross the touch' check
  is sometimes made against our own order.
- **Queue position.** 'At' gives our order the whole print at its price first. One tick inside the maker we are usually alone
  at that price, so this is closer to true there than for the ladder.
- **Coins for the asks** are assumed always there, as in every PR5-OPT replay (the executor tops them up).
- **One window.** 33 days, 15 of them in the wide regime, and the threshold was chosen after seeing it.

## 6. The twin, if Davies wants it

Not built. The rule, as it would be pre-registered:
- Paper twin of LIVE's rules on USDT-GBP at £600, compounding from its start like the other twins.
- One extra order a side, the size of the 0.3 % rung (£100), one tick inside the maker's order (the best level of
  4,999.5-5,010.5 coins in one order, or 4,999.5-6,000 coins in several), at least 2 bps from fair and not crossing the touch,
  only while the maker's bid and ask are 10 ticks or more apart in a snapshot seen within 75 s. While it rests or holds, the
  0.3 % rung quotes no entry; while the 0.3 % rung holds, it rests nothing. Its exit is the rule's (fair and one tick).
- It needs the twin engine to read `agent_book_levels` each minute. The frozen twin code (`quotes_twin.ts`) cannot change, so
  it would be a new driver beside it.
- Judged against LIVE's twin over the same days, paired by UTC day in bps of capital, read only on days the maker was wide.
