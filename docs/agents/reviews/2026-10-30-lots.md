# LOTS: more than one lot a rung? (2026-10-30)

Davies, 2026-10-30 ~04:30 UTC: "既然影响我们收益最大的因素是等待，目前我们每档只有一个lot，如果我们每档多做几个呢？或者设计下每档多少个？
会不会提升收益？你也仔细深度研究下，在live的这三个策略上，也可以挑一些你觉得有代表性的testing策略".

**Not blind, research only.** No order, config, migration or frozen file was touched. Every pound below is a replay's (paper).
LIVE's own fill prints were taken out of the tape by id, and that list is not committed. Script and results:
`docs/agents/backtests/mmcomp/scripts/lots.py` -> `results/lots.json`. It follows `reviews/2026-10-30-maker-absent.md`.

## 0. Answers first

**1. More lots a rung: no. Keep one lot a rung (K = 1) on all three live strategies and on the twins tried.** Stacking lots
out of the same capital (a rung's £100 as two £50 lots, the second resting while the first waits for its exit) loses
30-75 % of the money on every strategy, under both fill rules and in both halves, with P(≤ 0) = 1.00 every time.

- **Why the waiting is not the lever.** The money comes from how much of each taker's print our order takes, not from how
  often a rung is free to quote.
  - The prints that fill us are mostly bigger than our order. A £100 order takes £100 of a print, and a £50 lot takes £50 of
    the same print.
  - Halving the order halves each bite. The rung being free sooner gives back only part of that: LIVE's trips go 6.9 -> 8.4 a
    day at seq2, but each is half the size.
  - Even while the other maker was away, when LIVE's bids were all held 31 % of the time, stacked lots of the same pounds
    earned less: £1.67 against £2.87 a day.
- **The stacked lots are also the worse ones.** A second lot fills when the price keeps going against the first. Its
  30-minute markout:

  | strategy, fills | first lot | second lot |
  |---|---|---|
  | LIVE, 'through' | 9.3 bps | 6.0 bps |
  | `live1200`, 'through' | 9.5 bps | 4.6 bps |
  | Coinbase, 'at' | 10.4 bps | 6.3 bps |

**2. "More capital" and "more lots" are different levers. Bigger rungs are the lever, more lots are not.** On LIVE's book,
with the extra £600 used two ways:

| LIVE, USDT-GBP | £ a day ('at' / 'through') | vs today, bps of £600 a day | P(≤ 0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| today: £600, one £100 lot a rung | 1.05 / 0.83 | | |
| £1,200 as one £200 lot a rung | **1.84 / 1.45** | +13.1 / +10.4 | 0.000 / 0.000 |
| £1,200 as two £100 lots a rung (stacked) | 1.22 / 0.96 | +2.8 / +2.2 | 0.000 / 0.000 |

- The same holds on every strategy tried. Doubled rungs earn 1.7-1.9 times as much; two stacked lots of today's size earn
  1.04-1.2 times.
- Both halves (before and from 10-13) and both fill rules agree, all at P 0.000.
- While the other maker is away, the doubled rungs earn +£4.82 over the absences (P 0.005). Two stacked lots earn +£0.29
  (P 0.36).
- `live1200` (variant-1, £1,200 from 10-28) is the test of this lever already running.

**3. One small thing does help, and it is not a reason to build anything yet.** The design "all": a rung's same pounds split
into 2-3 lots that all rest at once.
- **Why it helps.** Today a rung partly filled for more than £1 holds, and the unfilled part of its order is withdrawn. 19 %
  of LIVE's round trips bought or sold less than their lot, and the lots were filled 89 % on average. Split into lots, the
  unfilled lots keep resting, and each lot exits and rests again by itself.
- **The gain:**
  - LIVE: +0.32 / +0.12 bps of £600 a day for 2 lots (P 0.001 / 0.075) and +0.49 / +0.23 for 3 (P 0.000 / 0.001). That is
    +£0.02-0.03 a day, 2-3 %.
  - Its twins on USDT-GBP gain about as much.
  - On the USDC books it is not significant: RX USDC/GBP P 0.05-0.18, Coinbase 'at' P 0.29-0.30.
  - Under 'through' fills it is not significant before 10-13 (P 0.11-0.25).
- **The cost.** Two or three times the orders: LIVE 157 a day -> 311 (2 lots, busiest day 682) or 466 (3 lots, busiest day
  1,020, over the executor's 900 entry limit). It also brings more 24-hour stops (LIVE 3 -> 5-7, RX USDC/GBP 6 -> 11-16 under
  'through').
- **Not robust across books, fill rules and halves, and too small for its cost, so no twin is built.**
- A cheaper form of the same effect would be to let a partly filled rung keep only its unfilled remainder resting: one extra
  order per partial fill. That is not replayed here, and it is the version to try if Davies wants that last 2-3 %.

## 1. What was replayed

- **Engine.** `run_lots` in `lots.py`: run2.py's executor (PR5-OPT's, LIVE's rules) with each rung split into lots. Each lot
  has its own entry fill, its own exit at the rule's price (fair and the offset), its own 24-hour stop and the £1 holding line.
  With one lot a rung it gives `sim.run`'s trips exactly: 243 of 243 on LIVE's rules, 175 of 175 on RX USDC/GBP's.
- **Designs, at the strategy's capital unless named:**
  - `seq K`: K lots of rung/K, one resting at a time (K = 2, 3, 4, 6).
  - `all K`: K lots of rung/K, all resting at once (K = 2, 3).
  - `pool`: lots of half a rung, up to four a rung, as long as a side of the book commits no more than its rungs' pounds
    together. A busy rung borrows what an idle rung leaves.
  - `cap2_K1`: one lot of twice the rung (more capital).
  - `cap2_seq2`: two lots of the full rung, stacked (more lots, the same doubled capital).
  - Exits: each lot's own order. `agg` counts a rung's lots re-priced to one price in one turn as one order; it saves under
    10 %.
- **Strategies:**
  - LIVE: USDT-GBP, £600, 0.1/0.2/0.3 % at £100.
  - RX USDC/GBP: £120, 0.1/0.2 % at £30.
  - Coinbase LIVE: USDC-GBP, £120, £20 a rung, exits at fair. Replayed on Coinbase's own prints (the recorder's per-minute
    summary) with fair = 1 / GBP-USD (Yahoo's minute bar, held through closures), 2026-10-10 -> 10-29. It leaves out the
    Revolut X nudge, the release blackout and the second-level rate. The design comparison is like for like, but its levels
    are not the arm's.
  - Two TESTING arms:
    - `live1200` (variant-1): LIVE's rules at £1,200, the more-capital lever as it is being tested.
    - `n600usdt` (variant-4): both books 1:1 at £50 a rung, to see whether stacking helps where the capital is spread thin.
  - No OKX twin: the committed OKX record (`fair_price_cb_okx/inputs`) is not the print record this engine fills on.
- **Record.** MAKER-ABSENT's: Revolut X 2026-09-26 -> 10-30 03:00, LIVE's 677 + 225 own fill prints taken out.
- **Statistics.** Paired by UTC day in bps of capital, circular 3-day block bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261030.
  Halves split at 10-13. Maker there or away from the watch's history (paired by absence window).

## 3. What else was checked

- **Waiting.** The share of turns a side had no lot quoting falls a little when lots are stacked (LIVE bids 9.5 % -> 7.9 %
  at seq2). That is far too little to make up for halving every bite.
- **Inventory.** Same-capital designs never hold more than today: LIVE at most £300 a side. Doubling the capital doubles it
  to £600.
- **Worst day.** K = 1 -5.4 bps of £600; £1,200 rungs -10.7. RX USDC/GBP's doubled rungs -24.5 / -35.0 bps of £120. No day
  comes near the 1 % loss stop (-100).
- **24-hour stops.** Unchanged by stacking; up with `all` (see §0).
- **Top-ups.** The asks' coin need follows the most held a side, so it is unchanged at the same capital and doubles with
  doubled rungs. The replay assumes the coin is there, as every PR5-OPT replay does.
- **Orders.** Each strategy's busiest day, against the executor's limit of 900 entry POSTs a UTC day:
  - LIVE: K = 1 342, seq 2 389, all 2 682, all 3 1,020.
  - RX USDC/GBP: all 3 717.
  - `n600usdt` (two books, one account): all 3 1,113 and 1,032.
  - Both Revolut X accounts are sub-accounts with no venue daily limit (MMCOMP-2's sources); the limit that binds is our own
    governor.

## 4. What the replay cannot model

- A bigger resting order changing what takers do: a taker who sees £200 at our price might trade more or less than one who
  sees £100. The replay gives us the prints that happened, up to their size. Since the other maker's 5,000-coin orders sat
  behind ours, most of those prints were bigger than our orders.
- Queue position among our own lots and others' orders at the same price: 'at' gives our lots the print first.
- Coinbase's arm only approximately (§1).
- 34 days of Revolut X and 19 of Coinbase.

## 5. Recommendation

- **K = 1 on LIVE, RX USDC/GBP and Coinbase LIVE.** No twin.
- **If more is to be earned, it is by bigger rungs**, which is Davies' call on capital: LIVE 1.75 times, RX USDC/GBP 1.7
  times, Coinbase 1.9 times on doubled rungs in this replay. `live1200` is measuring that lever forward from 10-28.
- The 2-3 % from keeping a partly filled rung's remainder resting is real on USDT-GBP but small. Worth a twin only in its
  one-extra-order form, and only if Davies wants it.
