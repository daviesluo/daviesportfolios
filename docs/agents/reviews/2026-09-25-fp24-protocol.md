# fp24 protocol: total native fees (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp23 are dead, including LS-FADE, RV-RATIO, AC-HOUR, AVG-SIZE,
PAIR-CORR, CLOSE-LOC, GAP-UP, VWAP-PREM, VOL-HHI and UP-SHARE. None of
their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No fee stamp on or after
2024-01-01 is stored. The 2024-01-01 BTC daily open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the nineteen closed rounds. Also not here:

* Hash rate. fp8 scored the lower tail of Coin Metrics `HashRate` and left
  the upper tail unscored. This rule does not read hash rate, difficulty,
  or miner revenue. A day is not selected because the network got faster.
* Active-address counts, exchange inflow and outflow, and MVRV. A fee is
  what was paid to get into a block, not how many addresses moved and not
  a valuation ratio.
* Spot quote volume, trade count, average trade size, the taker share, and
  the hourly concentration of quote volume. No kline volume field is an
  input. The spot file stores the open, because that is the fill.
* The share of hours that closed up, the overnight gap, where the close
  sits in the range, and the close versus VWAP.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**FEE-HIGH.** One series: Coin Metrics community `FeeTotNtv` for `btc`,
daily. The signal day is the UTC date of `time` (the first ten characters,
`YYYY-MM-DD`). The value is the total transaction fees that day, in BTC.
A non-positive value is missing. A duplicate stamp is missing. A stamp
on or after 2024-01-01 is refused.

It fires when the fee is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier positive prints in `[D−90 days, D)`,
percentile `sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily
open. Hold one day. The spot open on the signal day is not the entry.
A cheap-fee day is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same fee series are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen. The mean fee and
the median fee are different series and are not neighbours.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
