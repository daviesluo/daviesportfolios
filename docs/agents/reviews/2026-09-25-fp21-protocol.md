# fp21 protocol: close versus the day's VWAP (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp20 are dead, including LS-FADE, RV-RATIO, AC-HOUR, AVG-SIZE,
PAIR-CORR, CLOSE-LOC and GAP-UP. None of their cuts is reused, and no
sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No daily bar opened after
2024-01-01 is pulled. The 2024-01-01 BTC open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the sixteen closed rounds. Also not here:

* The overnight gap. This rule does not read the previous close, and it
  does not read today's open. GAP-UP stays closed, and a gap down is not
  a candidate.
* Where the close sits in the high-low range. This rule does not read the
  high or the low. CLOSE-LOC stays closed.
* The width of the day's range. The quiet-day rule stays closed.
* Average trade size. That was quote volume divided by the trade count.
  This is quote volume divided by base volume, a price. The trade count
  is not an input, and neither is the level of volume: scaling base and
  quote by the same factor leaves the statistic unchanged.
* The taker share.
* BTC–ETH correlation, lag-1 autocorrelation of hourly returns, dispersion,
  skew, and the 7-day to 30-day realized-vol ratio.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**VWAP-PREM.** BTCUSDT daily bars. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01,
the VWAP is that day's quote volume divided by its base volume. The
statistic is the close divided by that VWAP, minus 1. The close, the base
volume and the quote volume must all be positive. A missing bar is a skip.
The open is not an input. The statistic is known when day D closes, and
its timestamp is D.

It fires when the premium is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. A close under the VWAP is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same premium are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
