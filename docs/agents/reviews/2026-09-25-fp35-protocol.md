# fp35 protocol: open versus yesterday's VWAP (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp29 are dead, including LS-FADE, BODY and its out-of-sample
result, BAL-CHG, IMPACT and its out-of-sample result, and PEAK. None of
their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped. IMPACT's 80th and 95th are
not candidates. BODY's 80th is not a candidate. PEAK's close under the
prior 30-day high, and its 80th, are not candidates. The level of the
address count is not a candidate, and neither is BAL-CHG's 80th.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No daily bar opens after 2024-01-01. The 2024-01-01 bar keeps its open and stores 0 for base volume and quote volume. The close is not stored.

## Not this search

* The same day's close versus that day's VWAP. VWAP-PREM stays closed.
* The overnight gap of this open versus the previous close. GAP-UP stays
  closed. The entry is the next open, not the open that is the signal.
* Distance from the prior 30-day close high. PEAK stays closed.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**OPEN-VWAP.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, yesterday is
D−1 day. Yesterday's VWAP is yesterday's quote volume divided by yesterday's
base volume. Both volumes must be positive, and today's open must be
positive. The statistic is today's open divided by that VWAP, minus 1.
Scaling yesterday's base and quote by the same positive constant leaves the
VWAP unchanged. A missing day is a skip. It is not filled. Today's close is
not an input. The statistic is known at today's open, and its timestamp is
D. The fill does not use that open: enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day.

It fires when the distance is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto
that can only reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored
on this screen.
