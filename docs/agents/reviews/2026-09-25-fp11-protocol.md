# fp11 protocol: perp book imbalance (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp10 are dead. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.
The lower tails closed in those rounds are not opened here.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No book file from 2024 onward is
pulled.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the six closed rounds. Also not here: cascade
bids and aggTrades wicks, the spot book, the 5% depth level, an ask-heavy
book, and a short of the perpetual. If the 1% level fails, the 5% level is
not the next candidate.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. A snapshot on or after 2024-01-01 is refused, except the
2024-01-01 BTC open, which may be an exit print. The null is fp5's: 200
draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, drawn from every in-screen BTC 8h hold.
A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above the
null.

## The idea

**DEPTH-BID.** Binance USDT-M book-depth files for BTCUSDT,
`data.binance.vision` daily `bookDepth`. Columns used: `timestamp`
(`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`, read as UTC), `percentage`, `notional`. Only
percentage −1 and +1 are kept. A snapshot at time t belongs to the 8h window
that starts at t rounded down to 00:00, 08:00 or 16:00 UTC. A snapshot exactly
on a boundary belongs to the window that starts there. Inside the window, the
later snapshot wins for each of the two percentages. If either side is
missing, or either notional is not positive, the window has no print. The
imbalance is (notional at −1% − notional at +1%) / (notional at −1% + notional
at +1%). It is known at the end of the window. That end is the print's
timestamp. It fires when the imbalance is strictly above its own
trailing-90-day 90th percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in
`[end−90 days, end)`, percentile `sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter
BTCUSDT spot at that end. Exit 8h later. The book is the perpetual's. The
position is spot. The ask-heavy tail is not scored. This is resting depth,
not a trade that printed through the book.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. Neighbouring quantiles, the
80th and the 95th of the same imbalance, are a veto that can only reject.
They are not scored on this screen. 2023 is not part of that bar. If this
rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the cut or the
1% level.
