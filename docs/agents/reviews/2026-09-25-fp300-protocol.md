## Where the fair value is published

The fair-value series is Coinbase Exchange `GET /products/BTC-USD/candles`
with `granularity=86400`, from `api.exchange.coinbase.com`. The public page
is `https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/reference/exchangerestapi_getproductcandles`.
Each row is `[time, low, high, open, close, volume]`. `time` is the bucket
start in Unix seconds. Every bucket in the pull used for the count starts at
00:00 UTC and the next starts 86,400 seconds later. The close is the last
trade in that bucket. It is published when the bucket ends, at `time + 86400`.
A decision at a Binance entry open uses a Coinbase close only when that end
is strictly earlier than the entry. The bucket that ends on the entry itself
is not used.

The price being judged is the finished close of the USDT book. That close is
not the fair value. The open of that book is the fill and is not an input.
Binance spot, the coin-margined book and the USDT book are not read as this
fair value.

## The idea

**CBWDN.** Fair value is the Coinbase Exchange BTC-USD daily close. The basis is the finished USDT close over that fair value. It finished positive and strictly above the basis five days earlier, so the next USDT open sells a premium to Coinbase that has widened. Sell the USDT book for eight days. The other trade sells the USDT book for eight days on these same prices when that wider premium is absent. Same side. The edge is turned off. Not the opposite side. Not an unconditional hold. The entry open is not an input. One leg.

The widening set is the shorter one at a strict premium: 132 against 226. No extra discount was required.
