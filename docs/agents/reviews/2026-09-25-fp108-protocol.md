## Not this search

* SOL, BNB, XRP, DOGE, ADA, AVAX, LINK or LTC quote volume over BTC quote volume. That family stops. Swapping the coin is not a new rule.
* The reciprocal, BTC quote over an alt quote.
* ETH quote over BTC quote, and BTC's share of BTC+ETH quote volume.
* The coin's own quote level, the trade-count level, and quote per trade.
* Taker-buy fields.
* Any hourly open, high, low, close, or hourly volume share from fp30 through fp94.
* The funding rate itself, its lower tail, and its upper tail. A day whose three prints sit high together is not this rule.
* The day's price range. A wide candle is not a wide funding range.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. Funding at or after 2024-01-01 is not requested. Daily opens
are stored through 2024-01-14 so this hold can exit. Those opens are not
inputs. A later open is a bug. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed
`20250925`, index 190, drawn from every in-screen BTCUSDT hold of 7
days. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above
the null.

## The idea

**FRNG-7.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, take the three
funding prints at 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC. The statistic is the highest
minus the lowest. A missing print, or any other funding timestamp on that
UTC day, is not a print. A zero range is a print and does not by itself
fire. The opens are stored for the fill and are not inputs. No hourly price
bar is read. The statistic is known at 16:00 UTC, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
7 days. The other seven holds in this round are their own rules, written
down with this one. A failed hold is not replaced by a neighbour hold after
the score.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic, at this same hold,
are named as neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only
after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any price after 2024-01-14. If this rule fails,
it is dead and the next search does not inherit the window, the hold, or the
cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto that can only
reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.
