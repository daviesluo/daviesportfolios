## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print and is not
filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of this same exit rule started on every in-screen day, whether or not the ratio crossed. Daily opens through 2024-01-05, and taker prints through 2024-01-04, may be used to finish a 2023 entry. They are not entries, and they are not a 2024 screen. A later open is a bug. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero,
and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**TAKX.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, the previous UTC date's last taker ratio is at or under one, and today's last ratio is strictly above one. A missing previous day is not a cross and is not filled in. The ratio is not ranked. The day-over-day jump stays closed. The level's tails stay closed.

Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. On each later day, read that day's last ratio. When it is at or under one, exit at the next daily open. If five daily observations have passed and it is still above one, exit at the open five days after entry. A missing observation drops the trade. The fill is fp5's `net_return` between the two opens. Five days is the cap written here, not a hold that was chosen after a return. Another fixed hold of this cross is not scored.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01 is scored. If
this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the hold or
the cut.
