# fp18 protocol: BTC–ETH return correlation (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp17 are dead, including LS-FADE, RV-RATIO, AC-HOUR and AVG-SIZE.
None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No daily bar opened after
2024-01-01 is pulled. The 2024-01-01 BTC open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the thirteen closed rounds. Also not here:

* Lag-1 autocorrelation of one coin's hours. This is the co-movement of two
  coins over 30 days. The lag is not reused, and no hourly bar is an input.
* ETH's last return as a lead, and the level of the ETH/BTC price. A single
  day's ETH move is not the signal. The position is BTC, not ETH.
* Cross-sectional dispersion and skew of the basket.
* Average trade size, the taker share, and the level of quote volume.
* The day's high-low range. This rule does not read high or low.
* The 7-day to 30-day realized-vol ratio, and DVOL.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**PAIR-CORR.** BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT daily bars. On day D, strictly before
2024-01-01, take the 30 close-to-close simple returns of each coin ending
on D. A return is that bar's close divided by the previous bar's close,
minus 1. Both closes must be positive, and every bar on that exact 24h
grid must exist for both coins. A missing bar is a skip.

The statistic is the population correlation of those two return lists.
The covariance and both variances divide by 30, not by 29. If either
variance is zero, the day is not a print. The correlation is known when
day D closes, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the correlation is strictly above its own trailing-90-day
90th percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. The low correlation — the two coins coming apart — is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same correlation are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
