# fp323 protocol: WBPAR (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. §3.369 is the
retrospective this round answers. This rule does not buy because a close
finished under another venue's close. Fair value and the finished close are
both BTC per WBTC. The discount is `(fair − close) / close`, in basis
points. The close is the previous day's bar on WBTCBTC. It has finished
before the entry. The entry open is the fill and is not in the subtraction.
The entry day's own close is not an input. High and low are not stored.

The fair value is the standing par of 1 BTC per WBTC. It is not a daily
print and it is not another venue's last trade. The issuer's page,
`https://wbtc.network/`, says "Every WBTC is backed 1:1 by Bitcoin in secure
custody". Those terms were already in force before every 2023 open. Spot,
the coin-margined book and the USDT book are not the fair value. A book
ticker is not read. The position is one leg. The count stays 30. A mean that
clears the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less is not taken out of sample.
Later years are scored only when the three screen bars pass and the mean net
is strictly more than 20 bps above the edge-off trade, and only in a later
file. LS-FADE's 60-trade reproduction, the control, doubled costs, and the
rule that no month is above 40% of the profit are not relaxed. A Binance
daily open is not a Revolut X fill.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption and is not a testing row. 2023 is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. Public
reads only. Ten basis points a side. One hundred dollars stands on the
one leg, so the dollar result is one hundred times that leg's net.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on the one leg. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC.
An exit may be the 2024-01-01 open when the hold reaches that day. That
open is not an entry and not a signal. A day with no print is not filled
in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of the edge-off trade
named below. When that trade has more fills than this rule, the cutoff is
the house p95 of samples of this rule's count. It is not replaced by that
trade's own mean. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and
mean net above that null.

A missing previous close is neither trade. It is not the edge-off set. The
par itself is not a print that can be missing.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE stays as written: both later sub-windows
positive, above a fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30
trades, no month above 40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more
than 4% a year on the $100 it locks. The 60-trade reproduction of LS-FADE
is not relaxed. A pass that clears the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less stays
inside 2023. If this rule fails, it is dead. This file does not arm a testing
row.

## Where the series is published

There is no daily fair-value file. The par is 1, the unit of the WBTCBTC
close. The page that states it is `https://wbtc.network/`. The daily bar of
`WBTCBTC` supplies the previous close and the two opens. It is read from the
vision spot daily kline, 366 rows from 2023-01-01 through the 2024-01-01
open. High and low are not stored. No book ticker is read.
`https://fapi.binance.com` and `https://dapi.binance.com` are not the host.
The vision host is `https://data.binance.vision`.

## The idea

**WBPAR.** Fair value is one BTC per WBTC. The previous close of WBTCBTC is
at least 1 basis point under that par. Buy the spot book for two days. The
other trade buys it for two days when the discount is below 1 basis point.
Same side. The edge is turned off. Not the opposite side. Not an
unconditional hold. The entry open is not an input. One leg.

Thresholds counted before this freeze, smallest first, were 1, 5, 10, 25,
50, 100, 200, 500, 1000 and 2000 basis points. The hold is two days. One
basis point is the first threshold at which the cheap set has at least 30
spans and the edge-off set is at least as long. Five basis points has 104
against 259, and ten basis points has 66 against 297. Both are this same
sentence and are not second rules.

A count of spans, taken before this freeze, with no profit computed: the rule
has 167 spans and the edge-off trade has 196. One fillable day has neither
print. Fillable days were 364.

USDCUSDT against a par of 1, and BETHETH against a par of 1, are this same
sentence on another token. They were counted and not shipped. At 1 basis
point USDCUSDT was the longer set, 185 against 109, and the quote is USDT
rather than the dollar the par is denominated in. BETHETH at 1 basis point
was 281 against 0. The detail is in §3.370.
