## The idea

**IDXSH.** On day D, strictly before 2023-12-31, the index close divided by the index open is strictly above the spot close divided by the spot open. Both ratios are known when D closes. An equal pair does not fire. This is not the spot close set against the index close.

Short the next BTCUSDT spot open and cover the following open. The fill sells at the fee under the entry open and buys at the fee over the later open.
