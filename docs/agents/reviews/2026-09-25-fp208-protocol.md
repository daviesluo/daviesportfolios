## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash is not added. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no
print is not a print and is not filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed
`20250925`, index 190, of the payoff named below on every in-screen day the
legs exist. That payoff is not an unconditional long. The strategy is that
payoff only after the idea below, so it is a subset. A pass is at least 30
trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above that null.

The null is a coin-margined short from the close to the next close, on every in-screen day. It is not an unconditional long.

## The idea

**MKUM.** On day D, strictly before 2023-12-31, the mark close divided by its open finished strictly above the USDT close divided by its open. Both returns are known when D closes. An equal pair does not fire. This is not the mark return set against the index return, and it is not the USDT close set against the mark close. The signal close is an input, so the sell is the next coin-margined close, not that close. The hold is one close to the next close, not two days and not three days. The high and the low are not read. A percentile is not used.

Sell the next coin-margined close and buy the following close. The fill sells at the fee under the first close and buys at the fee over the next close. Funding cash is not added. The coin-margined close on 2024-01-01 is an exit only. It is not a signal and not an entry. No day after 2024-01-01 is stored.

A count taken before this freeze, with no profit computed, was 180 days.
The rule is not widened if the scored count differs and stays at least 30.
If it is under 30, the rule fails as written.
