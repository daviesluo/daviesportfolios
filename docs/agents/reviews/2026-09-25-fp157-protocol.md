## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on each price leg. No spread on this screen. Funding
cash, where this file adds it, is not charged a fee. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. A day with no print is not a print and is not
filled in. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of every in-screen BTCUSDT hold from an open to the open two days later. The 2024-01-02 open may be that exit. It is not an entry, and it is not a 2024 screen. A later open is a bug. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero,
and mean net above that null.

## The idea

**MOM5.** On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, compare the BTCUSDT close with the close five UTC days earlier. It fires when today's close is strictly above that earlier close. A missing earlier close is not a print and is not filled in. The one-day return is not the statistic. DRET stays closed. No percentile is computed. The five days are the momentum horizon. They are not a shortened trailing window.

Enter the next daily open. Exit at the open two days after the entry. The fill is fp5's `net_return` on those two opens. The closes are the signal and are not the fill. An entry on 2023-12-31 exits at the 2024-01-02 open. That open is an exit print only.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01 is scored. If
this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the hold or
the cut.
