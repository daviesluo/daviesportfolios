# fp321 protocol: EREAL (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. §3.369 is the
retrospective this round answers. The previous eight were two funding
sentences copied across books. A funding rate is not a price, so the open's
discount in basis points was never stated. This rule states that discount.

Fair value and the finished close are both USD prices. The discount is
`(fair − close) / close`, in basis points. The close is the previous day's
bar on the contract being traded. It has finished before the entry. The
entry open is the fill and is not in the subtraction. The entry day's own
close is not an input. High and low are not stored.

The fair value is not another venue's last trade of ETH. Spot, the
coin-margined book and the USDT book are not each other's fair value. A
print that was not yet published at the decision is not used. A book ticker
is not read. The position is one leg. The count stays 30. A mean that clears
the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less is not taken out of sample. Later years
are scored only when the three screen bars pass and the mean net is strictly
more than 20 bps above the edge-off trade, and only in a later file.
LS-FADE's 60-trade reproduction, the control, doubled costs, and the rule
that no month is above 40% of the profit are not relaxed. A Binance daily
open is not a Revolut X fill.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption and is not a testing row. 2023 is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. Public
reads only. Ten basis points a side. One hundred dollars stands on the
one leg, so the dollar result is one hundred times that leg's net.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side on the one leg. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC.
An exit may be the 2024-01-01 open when the hold reaches that day. That
open is not an entry and not a signal. A day with no print is not filled
in, and it is not borrowed from another contract. The 2023-08-28 through
2023-08-31 gap on the coin-margined book is not filled in. The null is 200
draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, of the edge-off trade named below. When
that trade has more fills than this rule, the cutoff is the house p95 of
samples of this rule's count. It is not replaced by that trade's own mean.
When the two sets have the same length, every draw is the full pool, so that
p95 equals the edge-off mean. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above
zero, and mean net above that null.

A missing previous close, or a fair value that was not yet published, is
neither trade. It is not the edge-off set.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE stays as written: both later sub-windows
positive, above a fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30
trades, no month above 40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more
than 4% a year on the $100 it locks. The 60-trade reproduction of LS-FADE
is not relaxed. A pass that clears the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less stays
inside 2023. If this rule fails, it is dead. This file does not arm a testing
row.

## Where the series is published

The realized price is not a community metric named `PriceRealUSD`. That name
is absent from the community catalog. It is computed from three community
metrics that are present:

`CapMrktCurUSD / CapMVRVCur / SplyCur`.

That is the realized price in USD. Market capitalization over MVRV is the
realized capitalization, and dividing by supply is a USD price per coin. On
the 2023-01-01 label, `PriceUSD / CapMVRVCur` matched this quotient. `PriceUSD`
and `ReferenceRateUSD` are market prices. They are not stored and they are
not the fair value.

The pull is `GET /v4/timeseries/asset-metrics` on
`https://community-api.coinmetrics.io`, asset `eth`, frequency `1d`, metrics
`CapMrktCurUSD`, `CapMVRVCur`, `SplyCur`, `AssetEODCompletionTime`, from
2022-11-01 through 2024-01-05. The community catalog is described at
`https://coinmetrics.io/community-network-data/` and the MVRV metric at
`https://docs.coinmetrics.io/asset-metrics/market/capmvrvcur`.

Publication is `AssetEODCompletionTime`, unix seconds, converted to
milliseconds. A stamp that is not strictly earlier than the entry is not
used. The metric's 00:00 label is not the publication. On this pull the
completion sat 25.26 to 158.76 hours after that label. The rule uses the
timestamp. It does not assume a next-midnight lag.

Rows whose completion is on or after 2024-01-01 are not stored. The frozen
shape is 425 publications. The first is 1667356018000, realized price
`1664.018814646614598368202939`. The last is 1703987405000, realized price
`1802.597021982496263427718988`.

The daily bar of `ETHUSD_PERP` supplies the previous close and the two
opens. It is read from the vision coin-margined daily kline. Open and close
are stored. High and low are not stored. No book ticker is read.
`https://fapi.binance.com` and `https://dapi.binance.com` return 451 from
the network this was read on, so they are not the host. The vision host is
`https://data.binance.vision`.

## The idea

**EREAL.** Fair value is the Coin Metrics realized ETH price in USD, published
at `AssetEODCompletionTime`. The previous close of ETHUSD_PERP is at least
1 basis point under that price. Buy the coin-margined book for two days. The
other trade buys it for two days when the discount is below 1 basis point.
Same side. The edge is turned off. Not the opposite side. Not an unconditional
hold. The entry open is not an input. One leg.

Thresholds counted before this freeze, smallest first, were 1, 5, 10, 25,
50, 100, 200, 500, 1000 and 2000 basis points. Holds counted were 2, 3, 5, 8
and 13 days. Two days is the shortest hold that is not one day. One basis
point is the first threshold at which the cheap set has at least 30 spans and
the edge-off set is at least as long. Five basis points selects the same 31
days, because the smallest discount in the set is 6.61 basis points. It is
not a second rule. Ten basis points has 30 cheap days and 326 edge-off days
and is not a second rule. The threshold is not raised after a return.

A count of spans, taken before this freeze, with no profit computed: the rule
has 31 spans and the edge-off trade has 325. Two fillable days have neither
print. Fillable days were 358 on the traded book. The 31 discounts, which are
not returns, run from 6.61 basis points to 2435.47, with the middle one at
468.61.

BTC's realized price against BTCUSD_PERP had 12 cheap days at 1 basis point
through a 13-day hold. It was not shipped, and the book was not switched to
BTCUSDT. A second realized-price coin would be this same sentence. The other
series that were counted and not shipped are in §3.370.
