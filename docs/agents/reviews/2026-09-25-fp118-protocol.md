## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. No signal at or after 2024-01-01 is requested. The 2024-01-01
daily open may be an exit print. A later open is a bug. The null is fp5's:
200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190, drawn from every in-screen BTCUSDT
hold of one day. The pool is BTC's, because the fill is BTC's. A pass is at
least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**TOPSTD.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, take every `sum_toptrader_long_short_ratio` in that UTC day's metrics file. The statistic is the population standard deviation of those prints divided by their mean. Variance is divided by the count of prints, not by one less. Fewer than two prints, a blank field, or any non-positive print is not a print. The last print alone is not the statistic. The raw prints are stored, and the standard deviation is not stored. Account-count ratios are not read.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile `sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold one day. The level of the same column is a different rule, in another file.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic, at this same one-day
hold, are named as neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only
after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. The half-spread, if this rule passes, is measured on BTCUSDT after the
pre-registration and before any daily open after 2024-01-01. If this rule
fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit the window, the hold,
or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto that can only
reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.
