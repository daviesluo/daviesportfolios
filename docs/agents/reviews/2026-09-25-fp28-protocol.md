# fp28 protocol: volume lined up with the move (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp27 are dead, including LS-FADE, BODY and its out-of-sample
result, and BAL-CHG. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.
BODY's 80th-percentile neighbor is not a candidate. The level of the
address count is not a candidate, and neither is BAL-CHG's 80th.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No hourly bar opened on or after
2024-01-01 is pulled. The 2024-01-01 BTC daily open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the twenty-three closed rounds. Also not here:

* The count of hours that closed up. A day of down hours can still line
  volume up with the size of the move. UP-SHARE stays closed.
* Lag-1 autocorrelation of the hourly returns. This statistic pairs quote
  volume with the absolute move. It does not multiply a return by the
  previous one. AC-HOUR stays closed.
* The Herfindahl of the hourly quote shares. The same volumes, paired with
  the moves the other way, change this statistic and leave the Herfindahl
  alone. VOL-HHI stays closed.
* The day's total quote volume, and an 8h drop that is also a volume
  spike. Scaling every hour's volume by the same constant does not move
  the correlation. VOL-CLIMAX stays closed.
* Average trade size. The trade count is not read.
* Where the close sits in the range, the candle body, a doji, the width
  of the range, and the overnight gap.
* Addresses with a balance, the daily change in that count, and addresses
  that moved. BAL-CHG stays closed. Its 80th is not a candidate, and the
  level of the count is not scored.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**IMPACT.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, take the 24
hourly bars whose open is D, D+1h, …, D+23h. All 24 must exist. Each bar
contributes its quote volume and the absolute value of its own close
divided by its own open, minus 1. The open and the close must be positive.
Quote volume must not be negative. The statistic is the population
correlation of those two sequences of 24: covariance over n, each variance
over n, then covariance divided by the square root of the product of the
variances. If either variance is zero, the day is not a print. A missing
hour is a skip. It is known when the 23:00 bar closes, and its timestamp
is D.

It fires when the correlation is strictly above its own trailing-90-day
90th percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. A day whose volume and moves do not line up is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same correlation are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
