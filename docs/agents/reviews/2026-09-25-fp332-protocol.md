# fp332 protocol: SPQTR (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The number this file
scores is the change in the delivery basis, in basis points, between the
entry opens and the expiry-day opens. The move in the spot price over that
window is not the result.

Who is wrong: the buyer of the front Binance USDT-M quarterly pays more
USDT, at that contract's own open, than the Binance spot open, for a coin
the contract is written to meet at delivery. Binance's delivery note says
a quarterly delivers on the last Friday of the quarter at 08:00:00 UTC, and
an open position is closed at the settlement price
(`https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/a3401595e1734084959c61491bc0dbe3`).
A published cash-and-carry scanner states the same mechanism on Binance's
own books: the delivery basis has to converge because the contract settles,
and a perpetual funding snapshot is not that trade
(`https://github.com/tkwsean/SpotFuturesArbitrage`). The settlement index
is not read here. The exit is the two contracts' own 00:00 UTC opens on
the delivery day, which is before 08:00.

The basis is `(future − spot) / spot`, both legs this venue's own prices,
in basis points. At the entry it is the two opens. At the exit it is the
two opens on the expiry day. Gross is that difference divided by 10,000.
Four fills at ten basis points each come off, so forty basis points come
off. What remains is the basis change after that round trip.

The signal is the previous day's closes. It has finished before the entry
open. The entry open is not in the signal. High and low are not stored.
Another venue's close is not an input. The premium index is not an input.
Coin-margined books are the same sentence with the margin changed and are
not scored. The perpetual against the same quarterly is the same delivery
sentence and is not scored. ETH against its own quarterly is the same
sentence with the coin changed and is not scored.

The count stays 30. A mean that is negative, or that clears the edge-off
trade by 20 bps or less, is not taken out of sample. Later years are scored
only when the mean net is positive and strictly more than 20 bps above the
edge-off trade, and only in a later file. LS-FADE's 60-trade reproduction,
the control, doubled costs, and the rule that no month is above 40% of the
profit are not relaxed. A Binance open is not a Revolut X fill. This screen
is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an adoption and is
not a testing row. 2023 is spent once this screen runs. Nothing here changes
a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. Public reads only. One hundred
dollars stands on the package, so the dollar result is one hundred times
the net of the basis change. Each signal day is one such package, including
when the holds overlap.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a fill. The package is short the quarterly and long the
spot, four fills, forty basis points. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)`
UTC. The front contract is the soonest expiry still after the entry day,
and that contract must have a bar on the signal day, the entry day, and
the expiry day. Spot must have those three bars too. An expired contract
is not the front. The 2024-03-29 expiry open is an exit for the entries
that roll onto `BTCUSDT_240329`. That open is not an entry and not a
signal. A day with a missing bar is neither trade. The null is 200 draws,
seed `20250925`, index 190, of the same package when the previous close
basis is below 100 basis points. When that set has more fills than this
rule, the cutoff is the house p95 of samples of this rule's count. It is
not replaced by that set's own mean. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean
net above zero, and mean net above that null.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE stays as written: both later sub-windows
positive, above a fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30
trades, no month above 40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more
than 4% a year on the $100 it locks. The 60-trade reproduction of LS-FADE
is not relaxed. A pass that clears the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less
stays inside 2023. If this rule fails, it is dead. This file does not arm
a testing row.

## The idea

**SPQTR.** The previous close basis is at least 100 basis points. Short
the front quarterly and long Binance spot until the expiry-day open. The
other trade is the same package when the previous close basis is below
100 basis points. Same side. The edge is turned off.

Thresholds counted before this freeze, with no profit computed, on the
365 fillable days. The fee is forty basis points, so a line below that
fee is not a candidate. The shipped line is the smallest grid point above
that fee at which the cheap set has at least 30 days and is strictly
shorter than the edge-off set. An off-grid threshold is not used.
