# fp334 protocol: FDCSH (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The number this file
scores is the funding cash credited to the long, in basis points of
notional, minus forty basis points. The move in the coin between the two
opens is not added. A negative funding rate is cash the short pays the
long. It is not a reason to be long the coin.

Who is wrong: the short of `TRBUSDT` pays the long a cash funding amount
at the settlement. Binance's own description of the rate is that payment:
when the perpetual trades below spot, shorts pay longs, and the payment
is a fraction of notional exchanged at the settlement
(`https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/introduction-to-binance-futures-funding-rates-360033525031`).
A published note of the same mechanism says a negative rate means shorts
are paying to stay short, and that payment is not a forecast that the
coin rises (`https://news.chainspot.io/2025/12/19/funding-is-not-a-signal-until-it-is/`).
The package is long the perpetual and short the spot, so the coin's open
is hedged and the cash is what is booked. The cash at the exit is zero:
the print has been paid, and the next print is a different settlement.

The cash in basis points is the funding rate times −10,000. Gross is that
cash divided by 10,000. Four fills at ten basis points each come off, so
forty basis points come off. What remains is the cash after that round
trip. The entry is the open one hour before the settlement, so the hedge
is on before the print. The exit bar is the open one hour after the
settlement, so the hedge can be closed. That later open's price is not in
the result.

The cash print is not the signal. A pin that changes the rate keeps the
same membership and changes the cash. The signal is the average premium of
Binance's own hourly closes over this funding interval only, excluding the
hour that is still open at the entry. Premium is `(perpetual close − spot
close) / spot close`. The interval length is the `funding_interval_hours`
column of the funding file. An eight-hour settlement uses the seven
completed hours. A four-hour settlement uses the three completed hours. A
window that reaches into the previous interval is the wrong window and is
not used. The premium index is not read. Another venue is not read.

The signal is on when that average is at least 50 basis points cheap
(−50 or lower). The same package is the other trade when the average is
not that cheap. A four-month read of the USDT-M funding files (March,
June, September, December 2023) did not produce a second symbol with
thirty such days. No second symbol is scored. Pooling names is not a
second mechanism. The positive-rate side of this same name never reached
25 basis points: the largest positive rate in the 2023 file is 6.9124
basis points, so the long never pays 25 basis points. That side is not a
second rule.

The count stays 30. A mean that is negative, or that clears the edge-off
trade by 20 bps or less, is not taken out of sample. Later years are scored
only when the mean net is positive and strictly more than 20 bps above the
edge-off trade, and only in a later file. LS-FADE's 60-trade reproduction,
the control, doubled costs, and the rule that no month is above 40% of the
profit are not relaxed. A Binance open is not a Revolut X fill. This screen
is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an adoption and is
not a testing row. 2023 is spent once this screen runs. Nothing here changes
a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. Public reads only. One hundred
dollars stands on the package, so the dollar result is one hundred times
the net of the cash. Each settlement is one such package.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a fill. The package is long the perpetual and short the
spot, four fills, forty basis points. Entries in `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)`
UTC. The 00:00 settlement on 2023-01-01 has its entry at 2022-12-31 23:00
and is not an entry. A settlement whose entry hour or exit hour is missing
on either book is neither trade. A settlement whose interval window is
missing an hour is neither trade. The null is 200 draws, seed `20250925`,
index 190, of the same package when the interval premium is not 50 basis
points cheap. When that set has more fills than this rule, the cutoff is
the house p95 of samples of this rule's count. It is not replaced by that
set's own mean. A pass is at least 30 trades, mean net above zero, and
mean net above that null.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE stays as written: both later sub-windows
positive, above a fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30
trades, no month above 40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more
than 4% a year on the $100 it locks. The 60-trade reproduction of LS-FADE
is not relaxed. A pass that clears the edge-off trade by 20 bps or less
stays inside 2023. If this rule fails, it is dead. This file does not arm
a testing row.

## The idea

**FDCSH.** The completed-interval premium is at least 50 basis points
cheap. Long the perpetual and short the spot across that one settlement.
The result is the cash. The other trade is the same package when that
premium is not 50 basis points cheap. Same side. The edge is turned off.

Thresholds counted before this freeze, with no profit computed, on the
900 settlements that have both hedges and a complete interval window.
The fee is forty basis points, so a line below that fee is not a
candidate. The shipped line is the smallest grid point above that fee at
which the cheap set has at least 30 distinct entry days and is strictly
shorter than the edge-off set. The cash print is not used to move the
threshold.
