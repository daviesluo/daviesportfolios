## Not this search

* The first hour's return. OPEN-DRIVE stays closed, including its later years and its positive neighbours. Hour 0 is not an input. A missing hour 0 does not drop the day.
* The last hour's return. LAST-HOUR stays closed. Hour 23 is not an input.
* The second 12 hours' return minus the first 12 hours' return. HALF-DIFF stays closed. Hours 8 through 15 overlap both halves, and a move that lives only in that overlap is this statistic.
* The 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 funding clocks. SEASON stays closed. This is one return from hour 8's open to hour 15's close, not three clocks.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**MID-RET.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, divide hour 15's close by hour 8's open and subtract 1. Both must be positive. Hours 8 through 15 must all exist. Hours outside that block are not read. The known 13:00 hole sits inside the block, so that day is not a print. It is not filled in. The statistic is known when hour 15 closes, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the statistic is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. A weak middle block is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same statistic are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut. The 80th and the 95th of this statistic are a veto
that can only reject, and only after a pre-registration. They are not scored
on this screen.
