## 1. The cost model, per arm (exact)

Every arm calls `backtest.ts`'s `run` unchanged (sha256 `31d27c7d82f8a94c…`).
Only the `Costs` object passed to it changes. Per side, a fill costs the fee
plus the half-spread (entry at next open × (1 + half-spread), exit at next open
× (1 − half-spread), the stop at min(level, open) × (1 − half-spread)); a round
trip therefore costs 2 × fee + the full spread.

`<COIN>USDT` pair's status is TRADING AND its 24 h quote volume at the START

## 2. Windows, tapes, stop rules, evaluations — the project's, unchanged

- Windows A, B, C, D exactly as `windowsOn` in `backtest_tape.ts` /
  `backtest_sui.ts` (each coin's own Coinbase series cut in thirds; C and D on the
  Kraken extension; 180-day in-sample floor), with `backtest_tape.ts`'s Kraken-tape
  coverage rule and overlap acceptance test.
- Tapes: `coinbase` (Kraken's bundle spliced strictly before the Coinbase series)
  and `kraken` (Kraken's own 4 h tape end to end).
- Stop rules: `shipped` (the 8 % floor, no intra-bar trail — what `tick.ts` runs)
  and `trail` (the floor plus the 3×ATR(14) intra-bar trail). Never averaged.
- Grid: fast 10/20/30 × slow 50/100/150 × ATR 2/3/4. Seeded = 20/100/3. Chosen =
  the in-sample best by ret / max(0.05, drawdown) on the arm's OWN costs. Plateau =
  share of the 27 points positive out of sample on the arm's own costs.

## 4. Part 2 — SUI's seat: §3.20's test, verbatim, re-run per arm

§3.20, verbatim:

> **One pre-registered test** decides a drop: SUI's rank among the five on the
> cost of removing it, fold by fold, with an exact rank-sum null; drop only if
> p(worse) < 0.05 on BOTH tapes.

`backtest_sui.ts`'s pre-registration, verbatim:

> PRIMARY test (the live seat): S3's exchangeability test on the cost of
> removing each member — Δ ret/DD of the sleeve without it, capital rescaled —
> under the SHIPPED stop, on BOTH backbone tapes. Under "SUI's seat is like
> any other member's" SUI's rank among the five is uniform in every fold; the
> null of the rank sum is enumerated exactly.
>   · p(worse) < 0.05 on both tapes  → the evidence says drop SUI from the live row
>   · p(better) < 0.05 on both tapes → the evidence supports the seat
>   · otherwise                      → the performance evidence cannot decide

Applied unchanged to each Binance arm, with ALL FIVE members priced at that arm's
cost (a row lives on one venue): the same span (2023-05-20 → 2026-09-20), the
same seven folds of ~174 days, both tapes; the verdict reads the `shipped` stop
only, `trail` is reported beside it. p(worse) is `rankSumTest`'s `pWorse`: the
exact probability, under the uniform-rank null, of a rank sum at least as large
as SUI's. "Last of five" is read from S2's whole-span slot P&L per dollar
(`backtest_sui.ts`'s contribution rank over S_full), per tape, `shipped`.

## 5. Part 3 — the screen and §4.15's bar, verbatim, at Binance cost

§4.15, verbatim:

> §3.7's four tests — positive out of sample on Revolut X costs, drawdown under
> 35 %, at least half the parameter grid positive out of sample, positive on
> Kraken costs — decided AVAX in and LINK, DOGE, ADA out. §3.8 tightened it: the
> four tests on BOTH walk-forward windows (parameters on the first two thirds with
> the last third out, and parameters on the first third with the middle third
> out), and a Revolut X UK book of at least $100k a day, because a bar judged on
> one year is itself a fit to that year.

§3.7's fourth test, verbatim: "(4) the same rule is positive on Kraken costs, so
the result is not an artefact of one fee schedule."

Applied to a Binance arm the way §4.16 applies it to a Kraken-only coin ("the four
tests on both windows on THAT venue's costs and a book on that venue of at least
$100k a day"): (1) positive out of sample on the Binance arm's costs; (2)
drawdown under 35 % on those costs; (3) at least half the grid positive out of
sample on those costs, the chosen point chosen in sample on those costs; (4)
positive on Kraken costs — §3.7's wording, unchanged, so the Binance bar is
exactly as strict on the second schedule as Revolut X's is; book: §1's Binance
book leg. SECONDARY, reported and never promoted: test (4) priced on Revolut X's
costs instead of Kraken's.

Universe: the 27 coins `COSTS` prices, through `backtest_tape.ts`'s acceptance
test; a coin is in a condition's population when both A and B are priced on the
tape(s) the condition names and the venue can price it.

Conditions: stop {shipped, trail} × parameters {seeded, chosen} × tapes {both
agreeing, coinbase, kraken} = 12, each for the venues revx, kraken, binance,
binanceBnb. **Headline: `shipped · seeded · both tapes agreeing`** — the
definition `sui.json`'s `barBaseRate` and §4.15's correction use. §3.8's own
regime (`trail · chosen · coinbase`) is reported as the continuity row.

"Clears the bar that did not clear it at Revolut X or Kraken cost" = clears both
A and B under a Binance arm, and clears both under NEITHER the `revx` venue nor
the `kraken` venue, in the same condition.

## 6. The chance count

Per venue per condition, over the population n (coins with A and B priced), with
kA and kB the coins clearing each window and k the coins clearing both:

- (a) §3.12's control, as `backtest_kraken.ts` computes it: "if a coin's two
  windows were independent draws, the number of coins clearing BOTH is (coins ×
  the share that cleared A × the share that cleared B)" — n × (kA/n) × (kB/n).
- (b) the exact version (§3.15 / `tape.json` §T3): P(≥ k) when each window's
  clearers are an independent uniform subset of the population of the size
  observed — the hypergeometric intersection.
- (c) §4.15's correction: p = clear coin-windows ÷ priced coin-windows over all of
  A–D; expected = p² × n (its published value: 26 of 93 = 0.280, 1.80 for 23 coins).

Reading rule: a venue's screen has found something only if k exceeds (a) AND
(b)'s P(≥ k) is under 0.05. Otherwise its passes are what chance gives, and no
coin is a candidate on the count.

## 8. Determinism

The full run (all arms) is run twice into separate output directories; the two
`binance.json` files must have the same sha256.

## 9. What would, and would not, be concluded

- Part 1 says how much of each coin's record is its cost. It is not a
  recommendation to move the row.
- Part 2: the rule in §4 decides, per arm; whatever it says is a statement about
  a row executed on Binance, not about the Revolut X row.
- Part 3: a coin clearing under a Binance arm is at most a candidate under the
  bar, subject to §6's count and to a paper record; the bar admits, it does not
  certify (§4.15).
- Nothing here is about whether Binance may serve this UK account, custody or
  funding.
