## The idea

**GLDPM.** Fair value is the LBMA Gold Price PM in US dollars per troy ounce, divided by the completed BUSD price. The previous close of PAXGBUSD is at least 50 basis points under that price. Buy the spot book for two days. The other trade buys it for two days when the discount is below 50 basis points. Same side. The edge is turned off.
