# fp20 protocol: the overnight gap (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp19 are dead, including LS-FADE, RV-RATIO, AC-HOUR, AVG-SIZE,
PAIR-CORR and CLOSE-LOC. None of their cuts is reused, and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No daily bar opened after
2024-01-01 is pulled. The 2024-01-01 BTC open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the fifteen closed rounds. Also not here:

* Where the close sits in the high-low range. This rule does not read the
  high or the low. CLOSE-LOC stays closed, and a close on the low is not
  a candidate.
* The day's close-to-close return. A large gap can be given back before
  the close, and a large intraday move can open unchanged.
* The width of the day's range. The quiet-day rule stays closed.
* BTC–ETH correlation, ETH's last return, and the ETH/BTC price level.
* Lag-1 autocorrelation of hourly returns. No hourly bar is an input.
* Average trade size, the taker share, and quote volume. This rule does
  not read volume or a trade count.
* Tokenised-stock overnight fades. The prices here are BTCUSDT against
  its own previous close.
* Cross-sectional dispersion and skew, and the 7-day to 30-day
  realized-vol ratio.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**GAP-UP.** BTCUSDT daily bars. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, the
statistic is the overnight gap: that day's open divided by the previous
day's close, minus 1. Both prices must be positive, and the previous bar
must exist on the exact 24h grid. A missing bar is a skip. The high, the
low and the close of day D are not inputs. The gap is known at the open
of D, and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the gap is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open, the
open after the one the gap was read from. Hold one day. The gap down is
not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same gap are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
