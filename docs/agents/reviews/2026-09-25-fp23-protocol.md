# fp23 protocol: the share of up hours (2026-09-25)

Written before any return of this rule was computed. The rules in fp5
through fp22 are dead, including LS-FADE, RV-RATIO, AC-HOUR, AVG-SIZE,
PAIR-CORR, CLOSE-LOC, GAP-UP, VWAP-PREM and VOL-HHI. None of their cuts
is reused, and no sign is flipped.

This screen is the only measurement this file allows. A pass is not an
adoption. It earns a pre-registration before any later year is scored. 2023
is spent once this screen runs.

Nothing here changes a frozen spec, arms a row, or sends an order. The fill
is fp5's `net_return`. Public reads only. No hourly bar opened on or after
2024-01-01 is pulled. The 2024-01-01 BTC daily open may be an exit print.

## Not this search

Everything already priced, plus the eighteen closed rounds. Also not here:

* Lag-1 autocorrelation of the same hourly returns. This statistic counts
  how many hours closed above their own open. It does not multiply a
  return by the previous one. AC-HOUR stays closed, and the negative
  autocorrelation is not a candidate.
* Which hour was up. The 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 season rule stays closed.
* The Herfindahl of hourly quote volume. No volume is an input. VOL-HHI
  stays closed, and an even day is not a candidate.
* The day's close-to-close return, the overnight gap, and where the close
  sits in the high-low range. Twenty-three small up hours and one crash
  still count as twenty-three up hours.
* The close versus VWAP, average trade size, the taker share, BTC–ETH
  correlation, dispersion, skew, and the 7-day to 30-day realized-vol ratio.

## Costs, window, null

Ten basis points a side. No spread on this screen. Entries in
`[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)` UTC. History for a threshold may start on
2022-10-01. The null is fp5's: 200 draws, seed `20250925`, index 190,
drawn from every in-screen BTC daily hold. A pass is at least 30 trades,
mean net above zero, and mean net above the null.

## The idea

**UP-SHARE.** BTCUSDT. On day D, strictly before 2024-01-01, take the 24
hourly bars whose open is D, D+1h, …, D+23h. All 24 must exist. Each
hour's return is its close divided by its own open, minus 1. The open and
the close must be positive. A flat hour, close equal to open, is not an
up hour. The statistic is the number of strictly positive hours divided
by 24. A missing hour is a skip. It is known when the 23:00 bar closes,
and its timestamp is D.

It fires when the share is strictly above its own trailing-90-day 90th
percentile, at least 90 earlier prints in `[D−90 days, D)`, percentile
`sorted[floor(0.90 × (n − 1))]`. Enter the next BTCUSDT daily open. Hold
one day. A day of mostly down hours is not scored.

The 80th and the 95th percentiles of the same share are named as
neighbours. They are a veto that can only reject, and only after a
pre-registration. They are not scored on this screen.

## What a pass becomes

The house bar used for LS-FADE: both later sub-windows positive, above a
fresh null, positive with doubled costs, at least 30 trades, no month above
40% of the profit and the rest positive, and more than 4% a year on the $100
it locks, entries 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. 2023 is not part of that
bar. If this rule fails, it is dead and the next search does not inherit
the window or the cut.
