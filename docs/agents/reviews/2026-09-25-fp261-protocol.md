## The idea

**LAGB.** The coin-margined book's finished eleven-day return is strictly below spot's finished eleven-day return. Sell the next USDT open and buy it back six days later. The entry open is not an input. One USDT leg.

The other trade sells the next coin-margined open and buys it back six days later, on the same entries. Both windows have to exist on their own books. It is not the USDT short with the comparison turned off.
