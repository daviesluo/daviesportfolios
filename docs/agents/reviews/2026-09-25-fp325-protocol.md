## The idea

**ECBFX.** Fair value is the ECB reference rate, US dollars per euro, divided by the completed BUSD price. The previous close of EURBUSD is at least 25 basis points under that price. Buy the spot book for two days. The other trade buys it for two days when the discount is below 25 basis points. Same side. The edge is turned off.
