Both books are bought only when both discounts are strictly positive. A zero discount does not fire.

The front quarterly is the same contract as the T-bill rule: the nearest expiry strictly more than one day after the UK open. The step count is (expiry minus the open) divided by 8 hours, and it is not rounded. The funding row and the future close must each be strictly before the UK open. A future candle published after expiry is dropped.
