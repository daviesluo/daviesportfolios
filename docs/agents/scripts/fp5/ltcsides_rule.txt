Two identical rows are one bar. A non-positive open, high, low, close, base volume, quote volume, taker-buy base volume, or taker-buy quote volume makes the day missing. Distance is price. Signal day D fires when the high minus the buy price is strictly less than the buy price minus the low, and the sell price minus the low is strictly less than the high minus the sell price. An equal distance on either leg does not fire. Do not swap the two legs.

Gross bps on a book = (exit open / entry open - 1) * 10000. A trip exists only when both opens are positive. A signal missing either open is not a trip.
Each signal is its own round trip. The position is not carried. If the next day is also a signal, the first trip exits at that day's open and the second trip enters at the same open. Both the exit and the new entry are charged. There is no netting.
