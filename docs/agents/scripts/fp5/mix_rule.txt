Family: long the next day when BTC spot quote volume jumped relative to the perpetual.
Frozen 2026-09-25 02:11:12 UTC, before any spot-to-perp volume ratio or next-day return of this rule was computed.
Not the volume-spike test (that was total volume, and whether that day's price move continued).
Not average trade size (quote volume divided by the trade count, and that rule was killed).
Not the futures taker buy/sell ratio.
The series is the day-over-day change in spot quote volume divided by perpetual quote volume.

Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by BTCUSDT volumes.
Spot quote volume is field 8 (1-based) of the Binance spot daily kline, BTCUSDT.
Perpetual quote volume is field 8 of the Binance USD-M futures daily kline, BTCUSDT.
The day's ratio is spot quote volume divided by perpetual quote volume. A ratio is missing unless both are positive.
The day's change is that ratio over the previous calendar day's ratio, minus one.
A change is missing unless both ratios exist and the previous ratio is positive.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC day when date D's change is strictly above that threshold. Otherwise flat.
Execution days are 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31 whose previous calendar day has a change and 90 earlier changes.
The public futures daily kline archive for BTCUSDT starts 2020-01, which is why the screen is 2021: it is the first year whose January can have 90 earlier changes. Do not move the screen into 2020.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2021. No other year is scored. If field 8 is absent, record the gap and do not pick another field.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a falling spot share. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2022.
