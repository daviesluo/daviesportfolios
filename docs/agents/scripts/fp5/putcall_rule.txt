Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in the Cboe equity put/call ratio is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 03:07:25 UTC, before any put/call change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not VIX. Not the fear-and-greed index. Not Deribit DVOL. Not the index put/call file and not the total put/call file. Not the call, put, or total volume columns. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this equity put/call ratio.
Series: Cboe equity put/call file, column P/C Ratio. The file starts 2006-11-01 and ends 2019-10-04. A missing print, including a weekend and a holiday, is not a level. The ratio is positive.
Change = ratio[D] / ratio[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its ratio is positive. A missing day is not filled from the previous printed session. A Monday print is not a signal when Sunday has no print.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2018-01-01 through 2018-12-31. The file starts 2006-11-01 and both spot books start 2017-08-17, so 2018 is the first January with a full year of spot closes and 90 earlier changes. No other year is scored. 2019 is not scored: the file ends 2019-10-04, and the year is not added.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2018. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a falling put/call ratio. Do not switch to the index file, the total file, or a volume column. Do not fill from the previous printed session. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2019. Do not drop a month.
