Fair value is strike divided by one minus (call premium minus put premium). Premiums are dimensionless coin fractions. The result is USD per coin. It is the synthetic forward, not a discounted spot. discount_bps = (fair_usd / uk_open - 1) * 10000. Both books are bought only when both discounts are strictly positive. A zero discount does not fire.
Who is wrong: the desk that sold the at-the-money call and bought the at-the-money put, or the reverse, on the nearest Friday expiry that sits more than 2 days and at most 9 days after publication. Carry over 2 to 9 days is about 8 to 15 bp, which is less than the 40 bp both-book cost, so a pure-carry gap does not pay the fee. A four-hour long does not sell the synthetic, so the trade earns the next open, not convergence to the forward.
