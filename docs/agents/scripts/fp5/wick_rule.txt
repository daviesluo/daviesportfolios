Family: long the next day when the BTC spot candle had an unusually long lower wick.
Frozen 2026-09-25 02:15:40 UTC, before any wick share or next-day return of this rule was computed.
Not the close-to-close continuation (that was the sign of the daily return, and it was killed).
Not a quiet range (that was the width of a 10-day range).
Not the BTC-ETH return gap.
The series is the lower wick as a share of that day's high-to-low range.

Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by one BTCUSDT spot candle.
Prices and the candle are Binance spot daily klines.
Fields locked here, before any file of this rule is opened: open is field 2, high is field 3, low is field 4, close is field 5, all 1-based.
The lower wick is the lesser of open and close, minus the low.
The day's share is that wick divided by high minus low.
A share is missing unless high is above low and all four fields exist.
Sort the previous 90 printed shares ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC day when date D's share is strictly above that threshold. Otherwise flat.
Execution days are 2018-01-01 through 2018-12-31 whose previous calendar day has a share and 90 earlier shares.
The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2018. No other year is scored. If a locked field is absent, record the gap and do not pick another field.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a short lower wick. Do not use the upper wick. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2019.
