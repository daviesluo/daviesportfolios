Not the coin-margined versus USDT-margined price ratio. Not the spot-versus-perp volume ratio. Not the US-session share of spot volume. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this volume ratio.
Series: Binance COIN-M BTCUSD_PERP daily klines and USD-M BTCUSDT daily klines. COIN-M quote volume is field 8 (1-based), the base-asset volume in BTC. USD-M volume is field 6 (1-based), the base-asset volume in BTC. Ratio = coin-margined field 8 / USDT-margined field 6. Missing unless both days exist and the USDT-margined volume is positive. A zero coin-margined volume is a level of zero. COIN-M daily files start 2020-08-11; 2020-07 is absent.
Change = ratio[D] / ratio[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its ratio is positive. A missing day is not filled.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31. The coin-margined archive starts 2020-08-11, so 2021 is the first January with 90 earlier changes. No other year is scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2021. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
