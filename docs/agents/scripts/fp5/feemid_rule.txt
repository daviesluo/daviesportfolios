Family: long the next open when today's Bitcoin transaction fees are strictly above the mean of the previous four days and strictly below the day five back.
Frozen before any open of this rule was joined to a return.
Public file: api.blockchain.info chart transaction-fees, format json, timespan all, sampled false. The chart name is Total Transaction Fees. The unit is BTC. The description is the total value of all transaction fees paid to miners, not including the coinbase value of block rewards. Each point's x is a Unix second. Every point in this archive is stamped at 00:00:00 UTC, and that stamp names the calendar day. A day with two different values is unusable. A missing day is not zero and is not filled in. A nonpositive fee is left out and is not filled. Those zeros run from 2009-01-17 through 2010-11-28 and none of them sit in the screen window. The value is one number for the day. It is not a kline. The open, the high, the low, and the close are not the signal. Fifteen-minute bars are not read. Six-hour bars are not read. Four-hour bars are not read. This is not a comparison of which klines on the same day are higher or lower. This is not the order of six-hour closes. This is not median confirmation time, and the fall after a higher day is not rerun. This is not hash rate, not the on-chain transaction count, not average block size, and not an address count. This is not estimated transaction volume. This is not a trailing quintile and not a 30-day median. Not funding. Not the OKX basis. Not a moving average, a breakout, or a candlestick pattern.
The series is Bitcoin's total transaction fees in BTC. The other coins are not the signal. An earlier day is read only as this series' own earlier day, never as a price.
Today is c0 and the five earlier days are c1 through c5, in BTC. Signal day D fires when 4 * c0 > c1 + c2 + c3 + c4 and c0 < c5. An equal mean does not fire. An equal day five back does not fire. Do not reverse this.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this one series.
Signal day D is a calendar day. The signal is known by the end of day D. The execution day is the next UTC calendar day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31. A signal on 2020-12-31 may enter on 2021-01-01. No 2022 signal is scored. Do not add 2022. The chart has a positive fee on every day from 2020-12-31 through 2021-12-30, and the lags this rule names are present. A spot daily bar exists on every day from 2020-12-31 through 2021-12-31 for BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT. The fill uses that daily open. Do not start the year later. The six dates that lack a full fifteen-minute set are not read, because this rule does not read a fifteen-minute bar.
Fill, decided before the result: enter at the open of the execution day and exit at the open of the next calendar day.
The open is field index 1 (0-based) of the Binance spot 1d kline for that book (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). Binance prints a daily bar every calendar day, so that open is the next tradable open. There is no US-session filter. Do not replace this open with a six-hour open or a fifteen-minute open. Do not change the fill of the confirmation-time fall after a higher day. Do not change the fill of the BNB close span that is strictly wider than the open span. Do not change the fill of the ETH taker-buy price that sits strictly below the open. Do not change the fill of the LTC weighted price that sits strictly below the midpoint of the high and the low. Do not change the fill of the OKX-minus-Binance basis. Do not rerun the coin-margined three-day trade-count rise on a different fill. The Revolut X archive does not contain those 2019 or 2021 fills. Do not build a paper-testing row for them.
Gross bps on a book = (exit open / entry open - 1) * 10000. A trip exists only when both opens are positive. A signal missing either open is not a trip.
Each signal is its own round trip. The position is not carried. If the next day is also a signal, the first trip exits at that day's open and the second trip enters at the same open. Both the exit and the new entry are charged. There is no netting.
Cost 20 bps on the entry and 20 bps on the exit. Stress doubles each charge to 40 and 40.
Both charges are booked on the entry day's calendar month. The exit open on the calendar day after the screen year is allowed for an entry on the last screen day. It is a fill, not an extra scored year.
Null: the same number of trips, random execution days in the screen year that have both opens, 500 draws, seed 20260925, index int(0.95*500)-1. The null is not loosened. Do not drop January from the XRP four-hour inversion rule, and do not change 8. Do not drop January from the confirmation-time rule with exactly two rising steps, and do not change 2. Do not lower the 60-trip gate because a six-hour rule had 49 trips or 26 trips, or because a confirmation-time rule had 33 trips.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = one entry at an execution-day open and the exit at the next day's open.
