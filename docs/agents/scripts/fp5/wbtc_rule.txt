Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in outstanding WBTC is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 02:32:28 UTC, before any WBTC change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not stablecoin-supply expansion (that rule was stablecoin market cap, and it had 10 entries). Not exchange net outflow. Not ETF creations. Not a dollar TVL. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by the WBTC token count.
Series: DefiLlama protocol wbtc, the tokens array, the WBTC key. Not tokensInUsd and not tvl. The session date is the UTC date of the date field.
Change = supply[D] / supply[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its supply is positive. A missing day is not filled.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2020-01-01 through 2020-12-31. The token-count series starts 2019-01-31, so 2020 is the first January with 90 earlier changes. 2021 is not scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2020. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a shrinking WBTC supply. Do not change the quintile. Do not use the dollar value. Do not add 2021. Do not drop a month.
