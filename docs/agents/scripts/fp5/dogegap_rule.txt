Not the DOGE close-change quintile. Not the DOGE quote-volume quintile. Not a trailing quintile. Not a flip. Not the OKX basis. Not the US-session share. Not the daily range.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this mechanism.
The DOGEUSDT spot open is field index 1 and the previous close is field index 4. Signal day D fires when D's open / previous calendar day's close - 1 is strictly greater than 0.01. Both prices must be positive. The gap is known at D's open. The books are still bought at the next calendar day's open, not at D's open. DOGEUSDT spot 1d starts 2019-07-05, so 2020 is the first full year in which 1 January already has a previous close.
The signal is known by the end of signal day D, or at D's open when the rule says so. The execution day is the next UTC calendar day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2020-01-01 through 2020-12-31. No other year is scored. No 2021 signal is scored.
Fill, decided before the result: enter at the open of the execution day and exit at the open of the next calendar day.
The open is field index 1 (0-based) of the Binance spot 1d kline for that book (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). Binance prints a daily bar every calendar day, so that open is the next tradable open. There is no US-session filter.
Gross bps on a book = (exit open / entry open - 1) * 10000. A trip exists only when both opens are positive. A signal missing either open is not a trip.
Each signal is its own round trip. The position is not carried. If the next day is also a signal, the first trip exits at that day's open and the second trip enters at the same open. Both the exit and the new entry are charged. There is no netting.
Cost 20 bps on the entry and 20 bps on the exit. Stress doubles each charge to 40 and 40.
Both charges are booked on the entry day's calendar month. The exit open on 2021-01-01 is allowed for an entry on 2020-12-31. It is a fill, not an extra scored year.
Null: the same number of trips, random execution days in 2020 that have both opens, 500 draws, seed 20260925, using these open-to-open returns and the same two charges. The null is not loosened. This is not a rerun of the LINK quote-volume screen.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
