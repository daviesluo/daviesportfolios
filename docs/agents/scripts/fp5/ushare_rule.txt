Not the return of the 15:00 UTC hour. Not a volume spike. Not the average trade size. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by BTC's volume share.
Series: Binance spot BTCUSDT hourly klines. Quote volume is field 8 (1-based). The US session is the bars whose open hour is 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 or 20 UTC. A day is a share only when all 24 open hours are present. Share = US quote volume / that day's quote volume. Missing when the total is not positive. Hourly files start 2017-08-17.
Change = share[D] / share[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its share is positive. A missing day is not filled.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2018-01-01 through 2018-12-31. Binance spot BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT both start in 2017-08, and the hourly archive starts 2017-08-17, so 2018 is the first January with 90 earlier changes. No other year is scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2018. No other year is scored.
