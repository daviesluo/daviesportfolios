Not BTC leading ETH (that was a print lead on one week, and it lost).
Not the absolute size of BTC's own move. Not VIX. Not a quiet range.
The series is the absolute difference of the two books' close-to-close returns.

Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both long on the same days.
A book's return on date D is that day's close over the previous close, minus one.
The day's divergence is the absolute difference of the BTC return and the ETH return.
A divergence is missing unless both returns exist.
Sort the previous 90 printed divergences ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC day when date D's divergence is strictly above that threshold. Otherwise flat.
Execution days are 2022-01-01 through 2022-12-31 whose previous calendar day has a divergence and 90 earlier divergences.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2022. No other year is scored. Do not replace the difference with BTC's own absolute return.
