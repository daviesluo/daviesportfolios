Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in Deribit BTC DVOL is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 02:26:44 UTC, before any DVOL change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not the VIX (that rule is equity implied vol, a 252-session level). Not the crypto fear-and-greed index. Not funding. Not a futures premium. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by the BTC DVOL close.
Series: Deribit public get_volatility_index_data, currency BTC, resolution 86400. Each row is timestamp, open, high, low, close. The level is the close, index 4. The session date is the UTC date of the timestamp.
Change = close[D] / close[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its close is positive.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2022-01-01 through 2022-12-31. The first daily bar is 2021-03-24, so 2022 is the first January with 90 earlier changes. 2021 is not scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2022. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a falling DVOL. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2023. Do not drop a month.
