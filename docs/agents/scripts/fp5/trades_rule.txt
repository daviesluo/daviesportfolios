Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in BTC spot trade count is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 03:07:25 UTC, before any trade-count change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not the average trade size. Not a quote-volume spike. Not the taker-buy share. Not the US-session share of volume. Not the coin-margined versus USDT volume ratio. Not the ETH versus BTC quote-volume ratio. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this trade count.
Series: Binance spot BTCUSDT daily klines. Trade count is field 9 (1-based). A zero count is a level of zero. The spot book starts 2017-08-17.
Change = count[D] / count[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its count is positive. A missing day is not filled.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2018-01-01 through 2018-12-31. Both spot books start 2017-08-17, so 2018 is the first January with 90 earlier changes. No other year is scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2018. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a falling trade count. Do not divide by the count to recover trade size. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2019. Do not drop a month.
