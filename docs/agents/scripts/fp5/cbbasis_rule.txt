Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in the Coinbase-minus-Binance BTC basis is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 05:53:33 UTC, before any basis change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not the BTC perpetual premium index. Not the coin-margined versus linear premium. Not the USDC premium. Not OKX. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this cross-exchange basis.
Coinbase product BTC-USD, candle granularity 86400. The candle is [time, low, high, open, close, volume]. Close is index 4. Time is unix seconds.
Binance close is field index 4 (0-based) of the BTCUSDT spot 1d kline.
The level is coinbase_close / binance_close - 1. A level is missing unless both closes are positive. The basis is signed, so the change is a difference.
Change = level[D] - level[previous calendar day]. Missing unless the previous calendar day has a level.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Coinbase BTC-USD prints in 2016 and Binance BTCUSDT starts 2017-08-17, so 2018 is the first January with 90 earlier changes.
Execution days are 2018-01-01 through 2018-12-31. No other year is scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2018. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a narrowing basis. Do not switch to the premium index, to volume, or to another venue. Do not fill a missing day. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2019.
