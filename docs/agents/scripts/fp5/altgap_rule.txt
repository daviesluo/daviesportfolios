A minute is two-sided when it has at least one buy print and one sell print.
Gap = (highest sell print - lowest buy print) / midpoint of those two, in bps.
Kill the family unless at least one book has 30 or more two-sided minutes and a median gap of 20 bps or more.
A book that clears this screen is not a pass. It only earns a later, pre-registered print test on other days.
