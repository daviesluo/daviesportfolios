Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in Great Britain national demand is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 05:21:47 UTC, before any demand change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not Henry Hub. Not WTI. Not a New York electricity file. Not the embedded wind or solar columns on this file. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this demand series.
Series: NESO historic demand, 2017 and 2018 files. Column ND only. The day is the settlement date, parsed as DD-MMM-YYYY, not shifted to UTC. The level is the mean of that day's positive ND readings. A day with fewer than 46 positive readings is not a level. A missing day is not filled.
Change = level[D] / level[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its level is positive.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2018-01-01 through 2018-12-31. No other year is scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2018. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
