Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in the broad nominal US dollar index is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 03:07:25 UTC, before any dollar-index change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not the 10-year yield. Not the high-yield spread. Not the pound. Not the discontinued trade-weighted index DTWEXM. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this dollar index.
Series: FRED DTWEXBGS, broad goods dollar index, nominal. The printed file starts 2006-01-02. A missing print, including a weekend and a holiday, is not a level. The level is positive.
Change = index[D] / index[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its index is positive. A missing day is not filled from the previous printed session. A Monday print is not a signal when Sunday has no print.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2018-01-01 through 2018-12-31. The index starts 2006-01-02 and both spot books start 2017-08-17, so 2018 is the first January with a full year of spot closes and 90 earlier changes. No other year is scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2018. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to a falling dollar. Do not switch to DTWEXM, the 10-year, or the pound. Do not fill from the previous printed session. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2019. Do not drop a month.
