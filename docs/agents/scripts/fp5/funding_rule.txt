Family: long the next day when BTC perpetual funding is in its own trailing bottom decile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 01:44:16 UTC, before any funding value or next-day return of this rule was computed.
Not the Deribit carry reading (that was the funding payment itself, one day, 0.945 bps).
Not H3 (that skipped trend-4h entries when the 7-day funding sum sat in the top fifth of 365 days, and it was scored on windows B, C and D).
Not the long/short-account rule (that series is count_long_short_ratio, and it was killed).
This rule is a standalone long. It does not filter trend-4h. It does not read the top of the funding distribution.

Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by BTCUSDT perpetual funding.
Series: data.binance.vision futures/um monthly fundingRate for BTCUSDT, column last_funding_rate.
A UTC day's funding sum is the sum of that column's prints whose calc_time falls on that date.
Days with fewer than three prints are missing, not zero.
Sort the previous 90 printed daily sums ascending. The threshold is the 9th (1-based), index 8.
Long the next UTC day when date D's sum is strictly below that threshold. Otherwise flat.
Execution days are 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31 whose previous calendar day has a sum and 90 earlier sums.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2021. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to high funding. Do not change the decile. Do not add 2022.
