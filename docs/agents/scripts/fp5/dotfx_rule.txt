Family: long the next open after the DOTUSDT funding sum crosses from non-positive to positive.

Not a trailing quintile of a funding difference. Not the DOT close, the DOT quote volume, or the DOT basis. Not the ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, LINK, LTC, or ADA funding sum. Not the BTC funding level. Not a flip. Not the OKX basis. Not the US-session share. Not the daily range.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this mechanism.
Binance USD-M fundingRate archive, symbol DOTUSDT. Columns are calc_time and last_funding_rate. A day is the UTC date of calc_time. The level is the sum of that day's last_funding_rate prints. A day with fewer than three prints has no level. Signal day D fires when D's sum is strictly positive and the previous calendar day's sum is present and less than or equal to zero. DOTUSDT funding starts 2020-08-20, so 2021 is the first full year.
The signal is known by the end of signal day D, or at D's open when the rule says so. The execution day is the next UTC calendar day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31. No other year is scored. No 2022 signal is scored.
Fill, decided before the result: enter at the open of the execution day and exit at the open of the next calendar day.
The open is field index 1 (0-based) of the Binance spot 1d kline for that book (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). Binance prints a daily bar every calendar day, so that open is the next tradable open. There is no US-session filter.
Gross bps on a book = (exit open / entry open - 1) * 10000. A trip exists only when both opens are positive. A signal missing either open is not a trip.
Each signal is its own round trip. The position is not carried. If the next day is also a signal, the first trip exits at that day's open and the second trip enters at the same open. Both the exit and the new entry are charged. There is no netting.
Cost 20 bps on the entry and 20 bps on the exit. Stress doubles each charge to 40 and 40.
Both charges are booked on the entry day's calendar month. The exit open on 2022-01-01 is allowed for an entry on 2021-12-31. It is a fill, not an extra scored year.
Null: the same number of trips, random execution days in 2021 that have both opens, 500 draws, seed 20260925, using these open-to-open returns and the same two charges. The null is not loosened. This is not a rerun of the LINK quote-volume screen.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = one entry at an execution-day open and the exit at the next day's open.
