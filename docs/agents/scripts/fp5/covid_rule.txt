Family: long the next UTC day when the day-over-day change in United States new confirmed COVID-19 cases is in its own trailing top quintile.
Frozen 2026-09-25 03:34:46 UTC, before any case change of this rule was joined to a next-day return.
Not a Wikipedia pageview. Not the fear-and-greed index. Not VIX. Not a flip of any of those.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this case series.
Series: JHU CSSE time_series_covid19_confirmed_global.csv, the single row whose country is US. The level is new cases, the first difference of cumulative confirmed counts. The file starts 2020-01-22 and ends 2023-03-09. Columns are consecutive calendar days. A non-positive new-case print is not a level.
Change = level[D] / level[previous calendar day] - 1. Missing unless the previous calendar day exists and its level is positive. A missing day is not filled.
Sort the previous 90 printed changes ascending. The threshold is the 72nd (1-based), index 71.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that change is strictly above the threshold.
A day whose previous calendar day has no change is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31. The cumulative series starts 2020-01-22, so 2021 is the first January with 90 earlier changes. No other year is scored.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
Screen only: 2021. No other year is scored.
Null: the same number of long days, random execution days in the screen, 500 draws, seed 20260925.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
Round trip = an entry from flat to long.
Do not flip the sign to falling cases. Do not switch to deaths, recoveries, or another country. Do not fill a missing day. Do not change the quintile. Do not add 2022. Do not drop a month. Do not score the discontinued tail.
