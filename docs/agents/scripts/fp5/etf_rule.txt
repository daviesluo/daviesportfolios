A number in parentheses, or inside a redFont span, is negative. A plain number is positive.
A blank cell or a dash is missing, not zero. The unit is the published millions of USD.
Sort the previous 20 printed totals ascending. The median is the average of the 10th and 11th (1-based), indexes 9 and 10.
Long the next UTC calendar day when that session's total is strictly above the median.
A day whose previous calendar day has no total is not an execution day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31 whose previous calendar day has a total and 20 earlier totals.
Prices are Binance spot daily closes (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). The long day earns that day's close over the previous close.
Cost 20 bps on each change from flat to long and 20 bps on each change from long to flat.
A position still long on the last screen day is charged the exit that day and earns no further return.
Stress doubles each charge. A loss kills the family. A gain is not a pass: these are daily closes, not prints through a price.
