Family: long the next open when the BTC, ETH, BNB, and LTC coin-margined term bases are all strictly positive.
Frozen before any open of this rule was joined to a return.
The series is the coin-margined term basis. On calendar day D the basis of a coin is that coin's near quarterly daily close divided by the same coin's perpetual daily close, minus one. Both closes are Binance coin-margined 1d klines. The close is field index 4 (0-based). The signal uses those two closes only. Do not read the open, high, or low. The near contract is the quarterly whose expiry date is the earliest expiry strictly after calendar day D. The expiry dates are 2021-03-26, 2021-06-25, 2021-09-24, 2021-12-31, and 2022-03-25, and the date codes are 210326, 210625, 210924, 211231, and 220325. On an expiry date the next contract is used. Coins read by this screen: BTC, ETH, BNB, LTC, XRP, LINK, ADA, DOT. A day with two rows and different closes is missing for that contract. Two rows with the same close are one bar. A missing near-contract close or a missing perpetual close makes that coin's basis missing. A close that is not strictly positive makes that coin's basis missing. A missing basis is not zero.
Signal day D fires when the BTC, ETH, BNB, and LTC bases are all defined and each is strictly positive. A zero on any of the four does not fire. If any of the four bases is missing, the day does not fire. The other coins' bases are not read. This is not one coin's basis strictly positive, and it is not the basis compared with its own previous days. Do not flip this into all four strictly negative.
Spot cannot short. Long or flat. Books: BTC and ETH, both driven by this term basis.
Signal day D is a calendar day. The signal is known by the end of day D. The execution day is the next UTC calendar day. The signal is not carried forward.
Execution days are 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31. A signal on 2020-12-31 may enter on 2021-01-01. No 2022 signal is scored. Do not add 2022.
Fill, decided before the result: enter at the open of the execution day and exit at the open of the next calendar day.
The open is field index 1 (0-based) of the Binance spot 1d kline for that book (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT). Binance prints a daily bar every calendar day, so that open is the next tradable open. There is no US-session filter. Do not replace this open with the perpetual's own open, and do not rerun the coin-margined three-day trade-count rise on a different fill.
Gross bps on a book = (exit open / entry open - 1) * 10000. A trip exists only when both opens are positive. A signal missing either open is not a trip.
Each signal is its own round trip. The position is not carried. If the next day is also a signal, the first trip exits at that day's open and the second trip enters at the same open. Both the exit and the new entry are charged. There is no netting.
Cost 20 bps on the entry and 20 bps on the exit. Stress doubles each charge to 40 and 40.
Both charges are booked on the entry day's calendar month. The exit open on the calendar day after the screen year is allowed for an entry on the last screen day. It is a fill, not an extra scored year.
Null: the same number of trips, random execution days in the screen year that have both opens, 500 draws, seed 20260925, index int(0.95*500)-1. The null is not loosened.
Kill unless pooled equal-weight P&L > 0, stress > 0, round trips >= 60, both books > 0,
P&L > null p95, no calendar month is more than 40% of pooled P&L, and pooled P&L exceeds 400 bps.
