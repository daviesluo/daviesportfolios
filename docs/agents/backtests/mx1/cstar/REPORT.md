# C* for MX-1: the chase at the rules' own decision minutes

Written by the main session from the measuring agent's hand-back of 2026-09-28 (the agent could not write this file
itself). Every table the answer rests on was generated by the scripts in this folder: `report_tables.md`,
`cstar_table.md`, `breakeven.md`, `compare_output.md`, `tables.md`, `results.json`. Nothing was written to the repo;
no production reads; no keys; Deno 1.46.3.

## Answer

**C\* = 110 bps for both sides**, valid with entries crossing at T = 15 minutes and rule exits at T = 60 minutes.

It is the smallest multiple of 10 at or above every per-coin 97.5 % bootstrap upper bound of the mean chase, at each
side's own deadline, over windows A–C (2023-08-22 → 2026-09-20), whether decisions are counted per rule or on MX-1's
unit (both rules pooled, one event per symbol, side and minute).

- Binding cell: `trend-4h` SOL entries at T = 15, 109.97 bps from 4 misses (all 4^4 resamples enumerated: 109.969).
- Per side: 110 for entries; 100 for exits (binding `trend-1h` ETH at T = 60, 95.91 from 5 misses).
- On the pooled MX-1 unit alone: 90 for both (entries SOL 80.36, exits ETH 84.92).
- 110 also covers the Student-t bounds of the pooled unit (max 99.61).

Not covered by 110:
- Entries crossing at T = 60 need 180 (pooled unit, SOL 178.03 from 4 misses) or 210 (`trend-4h` SOL, 207.79 from 2).
- Window D (2022-08-22 → 2023-08-21) raises the exits: A–D pooled unit BTC 135.34 → 140; `trend-1h` BTC 195.47 → 200.
  Both from one exit, `trend-1h` BTC at the 2023-03-22 19:00 UTC close: 218.7 bps at 15 min, 484.9 at 60. Entries at
  T = 15 are unchanged by D (pooled 75.86, per rule 109.97).

Decision minutes vs ordinary closes (A–C): entries at T = 15 miss more often (11.2 % vs 8.0 %, z = +2.35) and chase
further when they miss (39.1 vs 25.4 bps, +13.7, 95 % bootstrap [+5.7, +23.7]). Exits do not: T = 60, 43.8 vs 53.3,
−9.5 [−22.1, +4.2].

Break-even miss rate at C\* = 110: 11/121 = 9.09 %. On Binance's tape at the decision minutes (A–C, pooled unit):
exits at T = 60 miss 5.0 % (BTC 6.6, ETH 4.5, SOL 3.3), under it; entries at T = 15 miss 11.2 % (BTC 15.7, ETH 8.5,
SOL 8.1), over it pooled and on BTC. At the measured mean chases (not the bound), mean A_T per event is positive in
every A–C cell: entries T = 15 pooled +5.40 bps; exits T = 60 +8.25 bps. Saving when filled: 11.0002 bps on every
filled buy, 10.9998 on every filled sell (1,881 filled events), i.e. 2h/(1∓h) + 9.

## Event definition

t0 = the decision bar's close; m0 = the open of the Binance 1 m kline starting at t0; h = 1 bp (s = 2 bps).
Buy: rests at P = m0(1−h); taker price K = m0(1+h). Filled if a minute starting in [t0, t0+T) has low < P (strictly);
otherwise chase = (open(t0+T)(1+h) − K)/K × 10^4. Sell mirrors it with high > P; chase = (K − open(t0+T)(1−h))/K × 10^4.
Bootstrap: 4,000 resamples, a fresh `random.Random(1)` per cell, `sorted(means)[int(0.975·4000)]`; p90 =
`sorted(c)[int(0.9n)]`. A_T = saving if filled, −chase if missed.

## Method

Decision times from the repo's own simulator: `runSplit` from `agents/backtest_fill.ts` (`run` plus a fill log), with
`backtest_testingset.ts`'s series and windows; seeded parameters, `COSTS.revx`, shipped stops (8 % floor, no intra-bar
trail, 2-bar cooldown); each window starts flat. All 24 cells (2 rules × 3 coins × A–D) match `testingset.json`'s
trades, stops, return and drawdown to 4 dp. Seven protective-floor stops (all `trend-4h`, all A–C) are excluded. The
two rules share 16 entry minutes in A–C, leaving 412 entry events and 419 exit events on MX-1's unit.

Tape: `data-api.binance.vision` 1-minute klines, 6,099 requests; one 80-minute gap (Binance outage, 2023-03-24
12:40–13:59 UTC) touches no decision minute. Hand checks of three decisions in plain Python match to 4 dp
(`handcheck_output.txt`); the event code reproduces the first measurement (`chase.py`) exactly.

## Caveats

Binance is not Revolut X's UK book: the fill test is a strict trade-through on the deepest tape, so UK miss rates at
these minutes are likely higher; the chase is a price move and should transfer. s = 2 bps for all coins (UK medians
BTC 1.60, ETH 1.83, SOL 3.47). The binding cells rest on 4–6 misses. The largest chases come from single hours, and a
constant from three years does not bound the next such hour. Decisions were computed on Coinbase's tape (the published
tables'); the loop decides on Kraken's candles.
