"In the fill's minute" uses the touch the turn read before the taker came, so it is a bound no real hedge reaches:
the realistic one is a minute later, after the information is in the book. As a strategy (quote, then hedge each fill),
a quoting day: S2 -47.6 $ of hedges against the -39.6 $ its fills actually lost; Q2 -18.7 against -14.4.
A hedge through a negative-risk set's other outcomes costs at least the same, since buying every other YES at its ask
locks $1 only as cheaply as selling at the bid, unless the set is mispriced (PMOPPS: 7 such episodes after fees in 8
days, $1.26 a day at $1,000).
