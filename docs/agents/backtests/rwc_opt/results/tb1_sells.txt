TB1-SELLS (2026-10-09): does live-prep's TB1 skip have to skip the SELLS of what it holds too?
=============================================================================================
Asked by Davies, 2026-10-09: "持有时卖单也不挂吗？之前研究的确定可以吗？" (a held Gemini 4.0 NO had no sell resting, because
pm_lp.ts's lpQuotes returns nothing at all when the raw touch is <= 1 tick, PM_LP_TIGHT, Addendum 2).

The earlier research, confirmed: RWC-OPT's simulator (scripts/sim.ts:307) and LPSELF's (../lpself/scripts/sim2.ts) run TB1
as tight {mode: "skip"}, which sets quoting = false for the whole market, so the sells of held tokens (reduce-first sells
of a selected market, and close-only sells of a carried one) were skipped in the tested rule exactly as the live path
skips them. A rule that skips only the buys was never tested before this file.

What was run (read-only; nothing of the live path changed, sim.ts / sim2.ts untouched, they stay frozen):
- scripts/tb1s_sim.ts: a copy of LPSELF's sim2.ts with one more mode, tight {mode: "skip-buys", maxTicks: 1}: in a tight
  minute only a side that is a SELL of a held token rests (>= N held under reduce-first, or a close-only sell of a
  carried market / under the stop); no buy rests; a tight minute with nothing to sell is "skip" exactly. A lone sell
  earns what the simulator's formula gives it (RW's min rule: nothing, as one side); close-only quotes earn nothing.
  Plus counters: holdings at cost each minute, tight minutes with a holding, and a fill log for mark-outs.
- scripts/tb1s_run.ts rw|pr [--at-price], scripts/tb1s_analyse.py, results/tb1s_*.json.
  L1 = live-prep's current rule as LPSELF's lpgrid.ts defines it (= RWC-OPT's C1): S2 (10 markets / $200, caps $320 /
  $100 a market, reduce-first, 5N, x2's pause 15 c / 60 min, the -$75 "paid" stop) + TB1 skip at one tick; carried
  markets by Phase A's passive model on RW's record (as C1 was judged), by close-only quotes on the full universe.
- The copy reproduces every committed series exactly (float equality, every day's total, rewards and stress): RWC-OPT's
  C1, S2 and C2-C1 (rw_arms.json, both fill models) and LPSELF's L1 and L0 (lp_rw*, lp_pr*.json).
- Records: RW's (2026-09-25 -> 10-08 23:10 UTC, 14 days) and LPSELF's full-universe pm-rec record (10-05 -> 10-08 22:59,
  4 days; data/pr_record.json is not committed, sha256 c855d12c...eaf2de9 as ../lpself/MANIFEST.json names it).
- Live-prep's own forward record: sql/tb1s_forward.sql on pm_lpprep_* (10-04 17:55 -> 10-09 01:32 UTC) through the
  Supabase connector, read-only, 2026-10-09 ~03:15 UTC. The live path's own fills since 01:32: 8, all buys, in 4 markets
  (to 03:17 UTC), so no live sell to read yet.

The direct effect is one-signed. The totals above mix the sells' own result with path effects (what a sell frees changes
every later cap, side and stop), whose sign flips with the exit model and the fill model. Measured directly, the sells
that skip-buys adds in tight minutes mark out worse than sells in other minutes on 5 of 6 runs, and lose money in 6 of 6:
  60-minute mark-out, cents a share in our favour      tight-minute sells      other sells (same arm)   $ of the tight sells
  RW passive, strict / at-price                        -4.26 / -2.13           -1.93 / -1.56            -29.85 / -31.71
  RW close-only, strict / at-price                     -4.17 / -3.05           -1.89 / -1.17            -27.55 / -35.69
  full universe, strict / at-price                     -4.34 / -1.51           -2.72 / -2.33            -11.39 / -11.68
  Live-prep's own paper fills (10-04 -> 10-09 01:32, before and after Addendum 2's deploy, judged by the stored touch):
  sells in one-tick minutes -4.03 c (37 fills, 521 shares, 13 markets, 25 market-hours) against -1.83 c for other sells
  (174 fills); a market-hour cluster bootstrap (seed 20261023, 2,000) puts the gap at -2.20 c, 90 % interval -4.20 to
  -0.08, worse in 96 % of draws; 19 of 23 tight clusters negative. (Buys go the other way: -0.91 c in tight minutes
  against -2.54 c, which is where Addendum 2's "one-tick minutes mark out better" figure came from.)
  A sell that does NOT fill costs nothing to wait: the held side's mid moves +0.12 c over 60 minutes after a tight minute
  with a sellable holding (10,624 minutes, 27 markets) and +0.19 c after other such minutes, i.e. no drift either way.

Inventory: letting the sells rest barely moves it, because they rarely fill (RW strict: 45 fills in 11,899 sellable tight
minutes). Held $ at cost, mean / max: RW passive strict L1 113.60 / 218.19, skip-buys 108.77 / 234.41; at-price 140.08 /
292.18 vs 142.46 / 303.28. Full universe strict 190.76 / 303.30 vs 184.55 / 288.48; at-price 191.27 / 324.97 vs 184.92 /
302.36. Tight minutes with a holding are common: under L1, RW strict 17,176 of 24,696 tight minutes held something and
13,671 held >= N (sellable); full universe 11,738 and 11,285 of 13,769.

Reading: in a one-tick book a resting sell is filled mostly when the price is running through it, the same adverse
selection that TB1 skips buys for; waiting costs nothing on average. The earlier research is right to skip the sells
as well. Recommendation: keep TB1 skipping sells (no change to pm_lp.ts). Confidence: moderate. The direct effect is
consistent across both records, both fill models and the forward paper record, but it is about $10-$35 a fortnight. The
totals are path-noisy (two of six R = 0.40 cells favour skip-buys), and the forward paper sample is 37 fills.
Not modelled: Polymarket's docs score a lone quote at a third when the mid is in [0.10, 0.90]; the simulator (RW's min rule)
gives a lone sell nothing, so skip-buys could earn some reward this file does not count (an upside bounded by the formula
at a third, for the few markets whose touch is tight; the min rule is what every earlier table used too).
